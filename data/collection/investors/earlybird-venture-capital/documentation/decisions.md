# Earlybird: investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../../COLLECTION.md` to Earlybird (slug
`earlybird-venture-capital`, display name **Earlybird**; the firm's full name
is Earlybird Venture Capital). The judge cites these rulings per line. Written
at kickoff 2026-10-06 (PRO-107, archetype B of the cancelled PRO-87 umbrella,
whose two derivations still apply) under the derivation protocol of
COLLECTION.md s1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to verified facts;
no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's), and **run-time standing
rulings** (dated, class-wide, flagged for review). PRO-107 named four deltas;
all four are derived below from verified facts (D2 to D4 the fund families,
D12 the registers east of Germany, D10 the German recipe, D11 the removed
entry-date window) and applied.

**The structural fact of this run:** Earlybird is a Berlin and Munich
early-stage firm founded in Hamburg in 1997. Its portfolio is European and
files almost nothing with the SEC, and until 2026 its own fund side was
invisible there too: the adviser registered as an exempt reporting adviser
only in 2026 (CRD 341941, with the Form D of Fund VIII). What carries the
base is the firm's own record across five site generations: the frames site
of 1999 to 2001, the 2002 to 2010 site with its A to Z portfolio list, its
exit lists and a press-release archive, the 2010 to 2013 site with one page
per company under Tech and Health Tech, the 2014 to 2024 site, and the live
Framer sites (earlybird.com: 120 companies with the firm's own invested year,
stage label, HQ and deal team; health.earlybird.com: 18). The name "Earlybird"
covered three fund families for a decade (Digital West, Digital East, Health);
which of them belong to this base is the boundary question of the run and is
derived first (D2 to D4).

- **D2: Digital East is OUT, partitioned as its own desk (T1; the T1 question
  PRO-107 named).** Facts, each read from the record on 2026-10-06:
  - **The firm's own words.** Nine company pages on earlybird.com carry the
    marker "Digital East Fund*" and the footnote "The investment decision for
    this company was made by the separate company Digital East Fund which was
    operating under the licensed 'Earlybird' brand." The imprint: "Responsible
    for investment decisions regarding the portfolio companies mentioned on
    this website are affiliated companies within the Earlybird Group or by
    licensees authorized to use the 'Earlybird' brand for venture capital
    services. In the latter case, a disclaimer is in place mentioning the
    licensee." The firm's post on Dan Lupu: "Previously, Dan was a Partner
    with the Earlybird-licensed Digital East Fund 2013 SCA Sicar and Digital
    East Fund II."
  - **Registration.** No Digital East vehicle is on the Berlin adviser's Form
    ADV. The Luxembourg register (RCS, via North Data) holds a separate tree at
    7 Avenue Gaston Diderich, L-1420 Luxembourg: Digital East Fund 2013 SCA
    SICAR (B169906, in liquidation), Earlybird Advisory Sarl (B179769, "the
    exclusive investment adviser to Earlybird Management S.A. ... in respect of
    the Digital East Fund 2013 SCA SICAR") and Digital East SCS (B180826, the
    founding partner vehicle). Earlybird Management SA files on EDGAR from the
    same address (CIK 1855761: Forms 3 and 4 of April 2021, the UiPath IPO).
  - **The brand was dropped.** Vestbee, 2024-11-13: "Bek Ventures, previously
    known as Earlybird Digital East Fund ... This August it confirmed that it
    would leave the Earlybird family after a decade as a brand licensee."
    bekventures.com presents UiPath and Payhawk as its own record ("a venture
    firm ... now in our second decade").
  **Derivation:** two standing rules decide it, in the same direction. A site
  listing suffices *unless the firm itself disclaims the position* (Erik,
  2026-09-17, on Menlo): here the firm disclaims the whole class in words, on
  the rows it still lists. And a desk counts IN where it operates under the
  firm's name and affiliates with it (Erik, 2026-09-28, on Headline), with the
  stated contrast that HongShan and Peak XV "dropped the brand, so they stay
  out": Digital East dropped the brand in 2024 and the firm now describes it
  as a separate company and a licensee. The registration map, which PRO-107
  asked for, says the same: its own Luxembourg fund, general partner and
  adviser, none of them under the Berlin adviser. Precedents: HongShan and
  Peak XV (separate firms since 2023, OUT with their earlier book); Lightspeed
  India (own registration and the firm's own "not a unitary enterprise"
  disclosure); Balderton D2 (a desk-era book belongs to the successor firm,
  here Bek Ventures).
  **Mechanics:** `config.json` `desks.partition: ["digital-east"]`; each row
  whose only Earlybird position is a Digital East Fund position is moved to
  that desk in `companies/identity-merges.json` (`desk`), so it leaves the
  published base as `other_desk`, preserved in `excluded.json` and reversible
  in one line. A row is a Digital East row when one of these holds: the firm's
  own marker; the archived site's own fund attribution (2013 to 2024);
  the deal team on the firm's record is Digital East's (Cem Sertoglu, Evren
  Ucok, Mehmet Atici, Dan Lupu before he joined Earlybird, or "Earlybird
  Digital East"); or Bek Ventures lists it and no Earlybird fund is named in
  any round.
  **What stays IN:** a company an Earlybird fund itself financed stays a
  member on that fund's entry, whatever Digital East did later (T2). Peak
  Games (2011, Roland Manger and Christian Nagel) and Socialbakers (2011,
  Jason Whitmire and Hendrik Brandis) predate the Digital East Fund (formed
  2012/2013) and are listed by the firm today without the marker: Fund IV
  positions, members.
  **Flagged for Erik** because of its size, not because it is open: it takes
  UiPath, Payhawk and the Turkish and CEE book out of this base. The reading
  that would bring them back (the brand at the time of the deal decides) is
  one line in `config.json`, and the rows are preserved for it.

- **D6: Vision Lab is OUT (T2, fund money).** Facts: "Earlybird Vision Lab is
  Germany's first VC incubator and impact fund supporting pre-seed startups by
  entrepreneurs with migration backgrounds", a six-month programme with "an
  investment through a convertible loan of EUR 25K" from the Vision Lab Fund;
  the firm calls it "a pro-bono impact initiative" beside its "two separate
  strategies". The vehicle, Earlybird Vision Lab GmbH & Co. KG (Munich HRA
  113170), has the management company (Earlybird VC Management GmbH & Co. KG)
  as its limited partner and eight sponsoring firms as former ones: the
  manager's and its sponsors' own money, not a fund of the firm, and none of
  the fifty cohort companies is on the firm's portfolio lists. **Derivation:**
  T2 asks whether a fund of the firm held the position; here none did, and the
  firm itself keeps the programme apart from its portfolio. `not_member`,
  unless a fund of the firm later invested, which is then the entry.
  Precedents: partners' own money (Cherry D2, standing since 2026-09-26);
  ICONIQ D9 (philanthropy is not an investment). Flagged for review.

- **D8: Venture entry, control and sale consideration (T3).** Primaries,

  it bought or merged with a company Earlybird backed is `not_member`, the
  backed company being the member. Named in advance, to be confirmed per row
  in the grind: "Sennder GmbH (ex Everoad S.A.S.)" (the firm's own label: the
  2017 entry is Everoad, merged into Sennder in 2020; Sennder is a member only
  if an Earlybird fund put new money into a Sennder financing). Where the
  firm's two names are one company it is an identity merge, not consideration:
  Myriad AG is Esmertec renamed after its 2009 merger with Purple Labs, both
  Earlybird companies; CrossLend GmbH (The Thing GmbH); Obligate (ex FQX);
  Simr (formerly UberCloud); Alokai (formerly Vue Storefront).

- **D9: Person or firm, and plumbing (T4, T5).** Standard seat dating; a
  roster-name Form D row needs affirmative corroboration and, lacking it, is
  `not_member`. Feeders, the Team, Carry, SLP, Holding and
  Vermoegensverwaltungs vehicles, the AIV and the Affiliates funds are
  vehicles, never rows. A position moved into a Growth Opportunities fund
  keeps its original entry; a new commitment made through one enters at that
  stage (Lightspeed's Project Mercury; Creandum's Select funds).

- **D10: German recipe (PRO-107 delta, derived).** Cherry's conventions apply:
  insolvency followed by an asset sale reads `acquired` with a successor note;
  a filing with no sale yet reads `defunct` (Erik, 2026-09-07); the home
  register's dissolution or insolvency flag is filing-grade `defunct`
  evidence. Non-USD figures convert at the ECB rate of their own date
  (`fx-convert.py`); a listing outside SEC registration (Interhyp was, NFON
  and Tipp24 / Zeal on Xetra, Myriad on SIX, Wilex / Heidelberg Pharma)
  takes a web-cited market cap.

- **D11: No entry-date window (PRO-107 delta, derived).** The issue expected
  "a pre-window slice under the 2006 constant"; the constant was removed on
  2026-09-19 (PRO-137). A 1998 entry is judged on the same evidence as a 2024
  one, and where the record does not give the round the stage reads
  `undetermined`. The 1999 to 2010 press archive on the firm's own site names
  many of those rounds and is read for every row it touches.

- **D12: Registers east of Germany (PRO-107 delta: decided in this run, never
  degraded in advance).** D2 takes most Turkish and CEE rows out of the
  published base, which changes what the probe is for and not whether it
  runs: the rows that stay (Peak Games in Istanbul, Socialbakers in Prague,
  and every CEE-headquartered row of the home funds) and the partitioned rows
  alike have a status the home register can settle. The probe, its budget and
  its verdict per country are in `sources/registers/cee-turkey-probe.json`
  and report.md; the finding is recorded either way.

- **D13: The firm's own stage and year fields (COLLECTION.md s5, s6).** The
  live list's "Stage" column is scored against the book's documented entry
  rounds before it labels anything; "Pre-Seed" already fails on sight (Aiven,
  Snyk, Isar Aerospace, B2X). The page's "Invested: YYYY" is the firm's own
  dated entry claim: a `first_seen` / `entry_date` anchor, read as the close
  year (Menlo rule), never a round word. The job board's stage is the
  vendor's and is never a label (standing).

## Run-time standing rulings

(dated, class-wide, flagged for review)

- **R1 (2026-10-07): "Pre-Fund Investments" are not members (T2 and T4 applied; flagged for
  review).** The firm's 2018 to 2023 list had a status of its own, "Pre-Fund Investments", beside
  Current and Exited. Research found no round naming an Earlybird fund for any of the thirteen
  home-desk rows under it: John Yianni's and Rainer Christine's earlier positions on the health
  side (Biocompatibles, Thermocore, Reverse Medical, CryoTherapeutics, Ayoxxa, Arthrokinetics,
  Topsfield Medical) and the Heilemann brothers' own companies and angel positions on the tech
  side (DailyDeal, Forto / FreightHub, JustBook, McMakler, test IO, Eyeota). The firm's own label
  is the evidence that the position began before the fund (COLLECTION.md s1, Erik 2026-09-26).
  The same reading takes out **Vitraum**, whose only round names Heilemann Ventures (the team
  joined Earlybird in April 2016). **Inkitt** and **CrossEngage** also began as Heilemann
  Ventures seeds, but an Earlybird fund is named in later rounds: they stay members and their
  entry is the fund's first round, which the record does not name (`undetermined`). The six
  Digital East rows under the same label (Trendyol, Yemeksepeti, GittiGidiyor, Grupanya,
  Propertyfinder, DoktorSitesi) are partitioned with their desk (D2).

- **R2 (2026-10-07): the sale-consideration class, as found (D8 applied; flagged for review).**
  Out, with the company Earlybird financed as the member: **BioValve** (Redeon, taken over in
  2001; the firm's own release: "Redeon (merged with Biovalve)"), **NoemaLife** (GMD, the 1999
  investment, merged in 2002; the firm's 2006 release describes only that path) and **Snyk**
  (DeepCode, bought in a share deal in September 2020; no Snyk round names Earlybird). **Sennder
  stays a member on its own entry**: beside the shares from the Everoad merger of June 2020,
  Earlybird put new money into Sennder's internal round that month (deutsche-startups and
  Reuters, 2020-06-09). Everoad and Sennder are two rows. Graviton (assets sold to Xsilogy for
  stock in 2003), GMD and Alloo are the financed companies themselves and stay.

- **R3 (2026-10-07): Lilium is out (T3, Erik 2026-09-06).** Earlybird led the PIPE of July 2023,
  22 months after the Nasdaq listing, and no Earlybird fund appears in a Lilium filing before it.

- **R4 (2026-10-07): the Form D name-match rows are all `not_member`, one ruling per person
  (T4).** Lionel Carnot's seats are Bay City Capital's (he joined Earlybird Health in 2016),
  Florent Gros's the Novartis Venture Fund's (joined 2020), Konstantin Guericke's are personal

- **R5 (2026-10-07): a round the record does not name reads `undetermined`, also where the firm
  describes it.** "First financing round", "second financing round", "initial round of venture

  no taxonomy stage (COLLECTION.md s6: the label follows what the record calls the round). 6Wunderkinder's
  entry was the purchase of High-Tech Gruenderfonds' stake in May 2012: a secondary, so Growth.

- **R6 (2026-10-07): the live list's "Stage" label is refused (D13).** Scored against the cited
  entry rounds: Series A 18 of 20, Seed 32 of 36, Series B 5 of 7, Pre-Seed 11 of 29 (66 of 92
  overall, `scratch/field-score.json`). The column carries no wording that it is the firm's entry
  round, "Pre-Seed" sits on Aiven, Isar Aerospace, N26, Peak Games and on 1999 to 2001 companies,
  and "Series B" is wrong on Black Forest Labs and Polares. It rides in the evidence pack and
  labels nothing; `site_api.entry_stage_fields` stays empty.

- **R7 (2026-10-07): graduation readings.** An unnamed later round graduates on its amount
  (BridgeCo's $13.3M second round of 2002; Taptalk's $5M of 2014; Terralayr's EUR 77M and EUR 192M
  rounds). Interhyp graduates on the later priced rounds the firm itself records (the July 2000

  A cited acquisition or shutdown decides a seed row whatever its age (Interaction, bought by
  Cognition ten months after its seed). Where the register dates the share issue earlier than
  the announcement, the register date is the entry (Keel: May 2022, announced October 2024).

- **R8 (2026-10-07): OneFootball reads below $1B, as in the Lakestar and USV books.** The only
  $1B claim is an unnamed-source "over $1 billion" at the April 2022 Series D: no figure, four
