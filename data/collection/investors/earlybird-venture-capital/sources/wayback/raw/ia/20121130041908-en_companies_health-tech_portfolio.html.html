Hemocompatible nanocoating for implants

Earlybird investment since 1999

The convergence of the medical technology and pharmaceutical fields is growing in significance with regard to treating patients. Hemoteq has become a leader in the development  of drug-eluting coating for medical devices. In cardiology, for example, drug-eluting coatings are already being used today for the coating of stents.
