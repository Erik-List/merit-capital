Precise localization of tumors within the patient is essential for the success of the therapy and for the reduction of side effects. Calypso has developed a GPS for the body that provides real-time tracking to keep radiation focused on the tumor, not the surrounding healthy tissue, thereby allowing for precise destruction of the tumor tissue.
