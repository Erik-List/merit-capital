www.wilex.com, Munich

Biomedical cancer therapies

Exit: 2006 IPO (Prime Standard Frankfurt Stock Exchange: WL6)

Wilex is a biopharmaceutical company that aims to develop drugs and diagnostics for the targeted detection and treatment of different cancer types, while at the same time maintaining a low side effect profile. Earlybird invested in Wilex during the second financing round. Beside its financial involvement, Earlybird also introduced the Rencarex project (now in phase III) through its contact to Centocor.
