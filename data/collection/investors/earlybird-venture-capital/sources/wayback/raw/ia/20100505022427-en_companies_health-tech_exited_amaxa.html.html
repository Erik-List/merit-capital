www.amaxa.com, Cologne

Non-viral gene transfer

Exit: Sold to Lonza Group Ltd. in 2008 (SWX: LONN)

Due to its extraordinary technology and its excellent management, amaxa became one of Germanys’ fastest-growing biotech companies. Earlybird discovered amaxa in 1998 in the first Business Plan Competition run by the newbusiness promotion network, NUK, in Cologne, and continuously supported the company until its exit. Earlybird and the amaxa team have together overcome several obstacles in the company’s evolution. For example, when the originally-planned product proved to be impractical they switched to Nucleofector®Technology which has since become a resounding success. Another common decision was an early venture into the U.S., the core market for biotechnology products. During the sale, Earybird recouped 13 times its original investment.
