Non-invasive cardiovascular monitoring

Exit: Sold to Edwards Lifesciences Corporation in 2012

In an emergency situation, the quick evaluation of a patients’ condition is vital for the appropriate therapy. BMEYE develops and distributes innovative medical technology that monitors non-invasive, cardiovascular parameters quickly and continually. Patients can be subjected to the correct therapy immediately. This helps to improve patient care and reduce healthcare costs. In 2012, Earlybird sold BMEYE to Edwards Lifesciences Corporation for US$ 42 million.
