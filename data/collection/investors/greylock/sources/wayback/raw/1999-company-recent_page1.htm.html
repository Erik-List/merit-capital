o give a flavor for the industries and kind of companies in which we are currently interested, several of our investments from the last year are highlighted below.
 

Authentica Securities Technology, Inc.
Authentica develops and sells fine-grained security software for documents and other intellectual property.

 

Corio
Corio was founded with the mission to deliver enterprise applications to growing mid-market companies. The company offers customers end-to-end enterprise application service solutions that lower the cost and take the risks out of business technology implementation and management. Greylock, along with Norwest and Peoplesoft, led the company’s Series B financing in April of 1999.

 

eForce
eForce is a start up professional services organization founded in 1999 by Nat Natraj, a veteran services executive formerly with Siebel Systems and Scopus. Greylock provided the company’s startup capital.

 

e-Steel
e-Steel is the premier Internet marketplace for the global steel industry. The company is led by Michael Levin, an experienced steel executive. Greylock, Bessemer Venture Partners and Kleiner Perkins led the company’s first round financing in June of 1999.

 

Idiom Technologies
Idiom Technologies provides software and services for globalizing Web sites and Web applications. Greylock, along with Sigma Partners and North Bridge venture Partners, led the company’s first round financing in January of 1999

 

MetaTel, Inc.
MetaTel is a early stage company focused on developing carrier class products for internet-based telephone services. Greylock backed Chris Lamb and Frank Slaughter, formerly of 3Com and Shiva respectively, in this seed investment.

 

MultiFlow Technologies LLC
MultiFlow Technologies is developing a complete Provisioning, Metering and Rating system for the broadband industry. Their products enable telecommunications companies to deliver new products faster and cheaper and leverage their network resources more efficiently. The company was founded by Abraham Gutman, an experienced executive from MediaOne. Greylock and Pilot House ventures led the company’s first round financing in the Spring of 1999.

 

Net Effect Systems
Net Effect Systems, formerly Extratis, is a startup software company developing real-time, interactive, web based customer support systems. The company is led by Julie Schoenfeld, formerly of Stream International, and Mike McFall, formerly of Inference and Integrated Analystics. Greylock and Trinity Ventures provided the company's first round of capital in June of 1998.
 

Octane Software, Inc.
Octane is an early stage company, founded by a team of executives from Scopus to provide application software and services to the rapidly emerging customer relationship management market. Greylock and Sigma Partners provided the company’s first round of capital in September of 1998.

 

OTG Software
OTG Software is a leading developer of automated data storage and document management software for Microsoft's Windows and Windows NT. The company's software is installed on nearly 40,000 seats worldwide. Greylock participated, with ABS Capital Partners, in a $12 million financing to fuel the company's growth.

 

PrairieComm, Inc.
PrairieComm, Inc. is an Illinois-based company providing chip set, embedded software, and licensable intellectual property (IP) to the wireless digital voice and data industry. Three talented executives from Motorola and Cadence founded the company in 1994 and Greylock led the company’s first external round of financing in the Spring of 1998.

 

Red Hat Software, Inc.
Red Hat is an established company, led by Robert Young, that develops and sells a commercial version of the Linux operating system – plus tools, documentation and services – to corporations and individuals. Greylock led a major expansion round of capital along with Benchmark, Intel and Netscape.

 

Send.com,
Send.com is an early stage business to consumer electronic commerce company focused on the making it fast and easy to send tasteful gifts. The company was founded by Mike Lannon, an experienced sales and marketing executive, and Greylock and Highland Capital led the company’s first round of venture capital along with Benchmark and Charles River Ventures.
 

Sightpath
Sightpath, formerly Clearview, is a startup company founded by former Avid Technology and Open Market executives with the objective of making it practical and affordable to publish and consume high-quality video over internet networks. Greylock led the company's first round of investment in June of 1998.

 

Sirocco Systems, Inc.
Sirocco Systems, Inc. develops leading edge, high-speed networking products for telecommunications service providers. The company was founded in January 1999 by a team of experienced networking professionals with a successful track record of developing innovative new networking products at companies such as Sahara Networks and Ascend. Greylock and Bessemer Venture Partners led the company’s first round financing.

 

SiTera,
SiTera, formerly Fusion Micromedia, is a fabless semiconductor company developing high performance, programmable Packet Processing Chip Sets for networking equipment. Greylock participated, along with Matrix, Interwest, and Sevin-Rosen in the company's $10 million first round financing.

 

Space.com,
Space.com will become the leading web site devoted to all aspects of space. The company is led by Lou Dobbs, formerly the president of CNNfn and the anchorperson of the popular news show, Moneyline. Greylock and venrock led the company’s first round investment in June of 1999.
 
 

Storage Networks, Inc.,
Storage Networks is a start up data services company founded by two seasoned veterans of the storage management industry, Peter Bell and Bill Miller. SNI is pioneering a new business model providing remote, on-line data storage services to Fortune 1000 class companies. Greylock participated, along with a cofounder of EMC and Sigma Partners, in the company’s first round of investment.
 

Student Advantage, Inc.
Student Advantage is a leading company in the United States for marketing to college students. The company offers a membership card discount program, a Web-based network with information and commerce, an electronic university newswire, as well as other media and marketing services for any company trying to reach the college student market.

 

Syndesis
Syndesis develops and sells enterprise software systems that enable telecommunications carriers to automate the end to end provisioning of data network services. Greylock and Seqouia Capital led a $17 million Series A financing to fund the company’s growth and expansion.
 

Tenor Networks, Inc.
Tenor Networks is a startup data communications company, developing high speed networking systems, founded by several experienced executives from 3Com and Synernetics. Greylock participated in the company’s first round of capital along with Matrix Partner and North Bridge Venture Partners.
