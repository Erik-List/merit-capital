Team

We Believe

We believe in building a partnership that leverages the collective power of complementary skill sets, distinct points of views and deep domain expertise that are as diverse as the founders we serve. In an industry that often glorifies individual investors and their egos, we believe that enduring success is shared success. At Lightspeed our teams' collective strengths and skills interlock together to become a force multiplier for the founders we serve.
