Global Presence

We believe in investing behind bold ideas and the founders brave enough to build them. We pursue those opportunities based on their merits regardless of where the founder resides. The future is global and so are we. 

The U.S., Europe and Israel locations are offices of Lightspeed Management Company, L.L.C. (“LSVP”); the India and Southeast Asia locations are offices of Lightspeed India Partners, L.L.P. (“LSIP”). LSVP and LSIP are distinct investment advisory entities, are not a unitary enterprise and operate their investment advisory businesses independently of one another.  From time to time LSVP may draw on its relationship with LSIP, and vice versa, but only to the extent consistent with its status as a separate investment adviser.
