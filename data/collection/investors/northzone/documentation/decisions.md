# Northzone — investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../../COLLECTION.md` to Northzone (slug
`northzone`, display name always **Northzone**; "Northzone Ventures" until the
2010s). The judge cites these rulings per line. Written at kickoff 2026-09-29
(PRO-105, sub-issue of the PRO-87 wave, archetype B) under the derivation
protocol of COLLECTION.md §1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to
verified facts; no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's), and
**run-time standing rulings** (dated, class-wide, flagged for review).

**The structural fact of this run:** Northzone is a European early-stage firm
founded in Norway in the 1990s, now run from London, Stockholm, Amsterdam,
Berlin and New York, whose portfolio is mostly European companies that file
nothing with the SEC. Its fund side is thinly visible there: six Jersey fund
filers on EDGAR (the oldest a 2015 Spotify vehicle) and three Jersey exempt
reporting advisers, each reporting one fund family. What carries the base:
(1) the firm's own record read in depth — the live Webflow list of 142
companies, each with a "Partnered" year, a status, a location, the deal
partner and a hidden Early/Growth word; the insights archive (689 posts); six
earlier site generations in the Internet Archive (2001–2025), among them a
press feed back to 2000 — (2) the exit-side EDGAR full-text sweep and the
Wikipedia sweep, (3) the home registers: Companies House (the firm's UK LLP
member register dates partner tenure exactly; status for UK rows),
Brønnøysund (status for Norwegian rows, and Northzone VII L.P.'s own Norwegian
registration, which is what the Norwegian shareholder register would key on),
and the Nordic registers for status.

- **D2 — Programmes and personal books (T2, fund money).** Venture partners
  (the 2009 site lists Ingar Østby and Karl-Christian Agerup as venture
  partners), executives in residence and advisers bring their own angel
  histories: personal, **OUT** unless a Northzone fund held the company.
  Partners' personal angel cheques OUT, and a founder-era company of a partner
  (Paul Murphy co-founded Dots before joining; Dots is a Northzone portfolio
  company on the firm's list, judged on the fund's own entry) is judged on
  fund money only. Scout and angel programmes: none found at kickoff;
  re-checked in the insights archive and the recall probe. Precedent: scout
  checks ruled OUT on Sequoia, a16z, Accel and Lightspeed; Cherry's
  founder-angel ruling (Erik 2026-09-04, generalised 2026-09-26).

- **D3 — Instruments (T3).** Standing rules verbatim: primaries and follow-ons
  IN; **secondaries and tenders IN as Growth entries**. **Northzone Streaming
  Opportunity L.P.** (2015, $7.5M sold to two investors, raised by the partners
  who held Spotify through the flagship funds) is a single-company vehicle of
  the firm: it is plumbing (D4), and a row whose only Northzone entry is through
  such a vehicle enters at the round the vehicle bought. **Northzone Conviction
  I** (2020) and the Growth funds invest beside the flagships: where the
  flagship already held the company, the entry stays the flagship's first
  round. Post-IPO or public-market purchases with no venture entry OUT
  (fuboTV: NZ VIII (GP) Ltd's 2020 13D follows the FaceBank merger; the row
  stands on the venture round before the merger if the record shows one, else
  it is judged under the post-RIA rule); LP stakes OUT; control positions and
  sponsor promotes OUT. Evidence sufficiency unchanged: a listing on any era
  of the firm's own site suffices alone; a fund vehicle on an SEC filing
  suffices alone; a Form D name-match never.

- **D4 — Plumbing and consideration (T5; §1, Erik 2026-09-22).** NZ X NIF
  L.P., the "NZ <fund> GP" vehicles, parallel and co-investment vehicles and
  the Streaming Opportunity vehicle invest beside their flagships: no separate
  rows. A holding that arrived as consideration for a portfolio company's
  shares is not a member: acquirers listed on the firm's site or on an exit
  filing (Take-Two for Dots, RadNet for Aidence, Silicon Labs for Energy Micro,
  ValueClick for PriceRunner, Ooyala for Videoplaza, IK Investment Partners for
  Episerver) are checked for consideration before they seed member.

- **D5 — Namesakes (T1, T5).** "North zone" is ordinary English in utility,
  grid and mining filings; the full-text phrase is the single token
  "Northzone", and its hits are read once for noise. Companies House holds
  unrelated "GM Northzone Limited" (14528592, Southend-on-Sea), "North Zone
  Trading Ltd" and "North Zone Merchandising Ltd": none shares the firm's
  office or officers. SPAC filings that name the firm only in a director's
  biography (Revelstone Capital, Marquee Raine) are not positions.

- **D6 — The firm's own per-company fields.** Each company page publishes
  **Partnered** (a year), **Founded**, a **status** (Active / Acquired / IPO),
  a **location** (country), the **Northzone Team** member(s), a **Unicorn**
  tag, a category and a hidden **Early / Growth** word. Partnered is the
  firm's own entry-year claim: a CLOSE-year anchor for `first_seen` and the
  censor clock, never an `entry_date` alone and never a stage (COLLECTION.md
  §6, "a bare year is an anchor"; the Menlo finding). The Early / Growth word
  is eligible only if it is the firm's claim about the round IT entered;
  "Early" straddles Seed and Series A and is an anchor whatever the score, and
  "Growth" labels only after the field is scored against documented entry
  rounds (§5, ≥95% over ≥30 rows for `high`). Klarna reads "Early" beside
  "Partnered 2015", which is not an early entry, so the field is suspected of
  describing the company or the fund family rather than the round; it is
  scored before any use. The status is status evidence overridden by a cited
  event (Point Nine R3); the location is first-party `hq_country` evidence;
  the team member is deal attribution; the Unicorn tag is the firm's label, a
  pointer to a priced round and never a mark (Rule A).

- **D7 — The press and insights archives are a first-party surface**
  (Creandum ruling 8, Point Nine D12, Balderton D12). The live insights (689
  posts), the 2015–2025 WordPress press and spotlight posts, the 2012–2014
  press releases, the 2009–2011 updates and the 2000–2008 Hugin releases
  (cws.huginonline.com/N/131881) name rounds and companies the live list
  omits. A post is class-1 evidence for the round it NAMES and asserts an
  entry only where it says so in words (COLLECTION.md §6, the Menlo finding);
  a company named in a post as a Northzone investment is `site_listing`-grade
  membership evidence.

- **D8 — FX, non-US listings, no window (policy constants).** Every EUR / SEK
  / NOK / DKK / GBP figure converts at the ECB reference rate of its own date
  via `scripts/fx-convert.py`, the native figure in the note; Oslo Børs
  (Kahoot!, Funcom, Asetek), Nasdaq Stockholm (Tobii, Footway), Euronext and
  other non-SEC listings take a web-cited market cap. **There is no
  entry-date window** (PRO-137, 2026-09-19): PRO-105's third point was written
  under the retired 2006 constant, so it re-derives cleanly — a 1997 entry is
  judged on the same evidence as a 2025 one, no row carries a window flag,
  and the report states the share of entries before 2006 as a plain count.

- **D9 — The roster (T4 seat dating).** Investing people over time from, in
  order of authority: the UK LLP member register (above, exact dates); the
  Northzone Ventures UK Limited directors; the related persons on the funds'
  own Form Ds (the 2015 Streaming Opportunity filing dates seven partners);
  the archived team pages 2003–2026 (`sources/wayback/partner-roster.json`,
  curated per person with a dated basis; the CDX sweep extends windows and
  never shortens a register-dated one); and the live team page (16 in the
  Investment group, 2026-09-29). Seats are dated against the person's firm at
  the time: Paul Murphy joined Northzone in February 2018 (LLP designated
  member 2019-04-06 → 2021-08-20) and announced his move to Lightspeed on
  2021-02-26 (TechCrunch, "Sources: Lightspeed is close to hiring a new
  London-based partner", 2021-02-26; his own post on X the same day), so his
  seats from March 2021 are Lightspeed's, and his Dots seat before 2018 is a
  founder's; a partner's seat at a prior or next firm is that firm's. Non-investing staff (platform, finance, legal, talent,
  operations) never attribute a seat.

- **D10 — Registers: status first, membership where the register answers
  it** (COLLECTION.md §5, the country table). Company status from the home
  register (dissolved, liquidation, bankruptcy) is filing-grade `defunct` /
  `acquired` evidence: Companies House for UK rows, Brønnøysund for Norwegian,
  Bolagsverket for Swedish, CVR for Danish, the Handelsregister for German.
  The UK officer graph corroborates and dates an entry, and is affirmative for
  an unlisted company only where the appointment's service address is a
  Northzone office or the seat passes between two Northzone people (Balderton
  D10). A fund of the firm on a company's share register (a UK confirmation
  statement; the Norwegian shareholder register, where Northzone VII L.P. is
  itself registered) is filing-grade membership evidence, class
  `fund_filing` (Balderton R3).

## Deltas (Erik's)

PRO-105 lists four points; none is a ruling no test covers.

1. *Three register regimes* (Nordic, UK, US) is a reporting instruction:
   coverage is reported per regime in `report.md`, not blended.
2. *Partial Form D visibility from the US office* is a test, run: the roster
   sweep matched 96 filings of 62 issuers on name alone at kickoff, judged
   person by person (§3), and its yield is reported.
3. *The pre-window slice* re-derives under PRO-137 (D8): no flags, a count.
4. *The Creandum overlap* is invariant 10: Creandum's published `base.csv` is
   read before the classification pass and the stale side aligned to the
   freshest mark.

So nothing is raised before kickoff. Traps: "north zone" in grid and mining
text (D5); an acquirer on an exit filing seeding a member (D4); a partner's
founder-era or next-firm seat (D9); the Early / Growth word read as a round
(D6).
