Verayo
Verayo is bringing to market a range of security and authentication solutions based on a breakthrough technology called Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs). PUFs make integrated circuits unclonable, allow them to be securely authenticated, and can generate a virtually unlimited number of unique cryptographic master keys from each integrated circuit.
