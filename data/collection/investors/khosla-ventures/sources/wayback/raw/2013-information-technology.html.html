Zyomed
Zyomed is developing a new generation of stand-off, non-invasive reliable sensors, and microsystems for personal and laboratory-scale medical diagnostics. Advanced signal processing is used in conjunction with tunable, biomodulated MEMS and photonic sensors for amplifying, quantitating, and tracking indirect spectral biomarkers associated with absolute changes in blood or tissue chemistry. Targeted applications include the monitoring of blood-glucose, subcutaneous inflammation and surgical site infections. Patient-centric, spatio-temporal spectral variations are accommodated via a robust self-calibration process. The fully-integrated diagnostic imaging system has an ultra-small footprint enabled by military and space-surveillance grade high-resolution spectroscopy sensors, especially within the non-invasive and minimally exploited mid- and long wavelength infrared as well as the terrahertz spectral regions.
