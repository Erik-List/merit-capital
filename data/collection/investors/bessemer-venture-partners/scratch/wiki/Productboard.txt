Productboard was launched at TechCrunch Disrupt in San Francisco in September 2016. Its platform linked customer requests and research to proposed features and product roadmaps. The company has offices in San Francisco and Prague.
In February 2022, Productboard raised US$125 million in a Series D round led by Dragoneer Investment Group and Tiger Global Management. The round brought its total funding to US$262 million and valued it at US$1.725 billion. In the same year, it acquired SatisMeter, a Prague-based company whose software collected feedback from users on websites and in mobile apps.


== Restructuring and AI development ==
Productboard cut approximately one-fifth of its workforce, or about 100 employees, in November 2022, citing weaker demand for software. Further job cuts were reported in January 2024.
 In April 2026, the company cut more than 30% of its workforce as it reorganized around Productboard Spark, its AI product.


== Software ==
Productboard's platform collects feedback from sources like customer-support systems, surveys and direct user research. Product teams can link that information to feature proposals, set priorities and create roadmaps.
Productboard Spark adds AI-assisted analysis and planning to the company's product-management software.
