Flixbus
- Jochen, Daniel and André built the world’s fastest-growing intercity bus operator.

On a ski trip in 2011, three close friends came up with an idea that has since grown into the world's largest intercity bus mobility provider. In 2022, Flix carried 60 million passengers between over 3,000 destinations in more than 40 countries in Europe, North America, and South America. A launch in India is coming next.

The trio embarked on a mission to make coach travel more affordable, greener — and, well, cool. They were helped when a clear opportunity emerged in 2013: Germany began deregulating the industry. This allowed intercity buses to compete with state-run subsidized railways, and to become the choice of a younger generation of cost-conscious travelers.

UiPath
- Daniel has built the fastest-growing and leading provider of Robotic Process Automation.

Daniel's journey from a small town in Romania to the CEO of UiPath, the juggernaut in Robotic Process Automation, is a tale of resilience and adaptability. Born in Onești, he aspired to be an author before discovering his calling in mathematics and computer science. After university, he supported himself in Romania's post-communist economy with currency arbitrage, and developed a passion for competitive bridge. He also taught himself to program, which led to five pivotal years as an engineer at Microsoft in Seattle.

Once he returned to Bucharest in 2005, Daniel founded DeskOver, which evolved into UiPath. He recognised the potential of the emerging RPA market and strategically positioned UiPath for a public offering in 2021 — a move that achieved a staggering $36 billion valuation.

That leadership in the RPA market has established Daniel as a pivotal figure in shaping how automation and human work integrate. Through it all, Daniel emphasizes a corporate culture of humility, a quality he says is essential for adaptation and innovation.

Personio
- Hanno and his team are revolutionizing human resources management steering HR to the digital age.

In just eight years, the software firm Personio has become Europe’s leading provider of HR software and one of the continent’s most valuable start-ups. Hanno Renner, the co-founder and CEO, remembers a time when money was far tighter. Before the company received its first real funding, it was down to just €200 in its bank account.

“As students, we didn’t have an office,” he recalls. “So we worked wherever we could find space at college to build Personio’s first software product — scrambling together any savings we had to help with the costs.”

Today Personio has more than 10,000 customers and 1,800 employees from more than 80 countries.

Pigment
- Eléonore and Romain are tackling only the most ambitious problems. They settled on a plan to replace spreadsheets — in effect, to liberate 1 billion users from the tyranny of Excel. Pigment‘s mission is to allow businesses everywhere to make better, faster decisions in a changing world - drawing on data analysis, intuitive design and sophisticated modelling to help leaders spend more time thinking and less time number-crunching.

Incredible willpower, scalpel-sharp business acumen, and imperturbable clarity of mission — these are some of Eleonore Crespo’s ingredients for success.

“Sometimes the most painful moments in life turn out to be the ones that push you to get started,” she says. “For me, while I was working in VC, I got an awful back injury that required surgery, and had me bedridden for months, putting my normal life on hold. In the middle of all this pain, I had the chance to think about what I really wanted in life, and the answer was clear: I wanted to build something big, something really impactful.”

She and her co-founder Romain agreed: They would tackle only the most ambitious problems. They settled on a plan to replace spreadsheets — in effect, to liberate 1 billion users from the tyranny of Excel. The result is Pigment, a platform businesses everywhere rely on to make better, faster decisions in a changing world. It enables leaders to draw on data analysis, intuitive design, and sophisticated modeling to help them spend more time thinking and less time crunching numbers.

In short order, Pigment signed such clients as Deliveroo, Klarna, Carta, Evenflo, Miro, Airtable, Figma, Brex, Webhelp, Gong, Poshmark, Eventbrite, and Mozilla. In the summer of 2023 the company closed a Series C round that put its total fundraise at $248 million in less than three years.

Wolt
- Miki is the co-founder & CEO of the Helsinki-based company Wolt, Europe’s second fastest growing company, known for its food delivery platform.

Miki Kuusi came of age in the aftermath of the 2009 global recession, a moment where Europe felt wide-open and the iPhone and Android had people rushing to smartphones. People were interacting with computers and the internet in novel ways, leading to a startlingly simple question that inspired Miki and five other co-founders to launch Wolt in 2014. If phones were being used to summon cars via apps such as Uber, could they do the same for food?

Supercell
- Ilkka is the CEO and co-founder of Supercell, the Helsinki-based mobile games developer behind Clash of Clans, Hay Day, Boom Beach, Clash Royale and Brawl Stars.

It’s not false modesty when llkka describes himself as the “least-powerful CEO in video games.” Rather, he is really offering an insight into his radically contrarian approach to start-ups and company building — one that has driven outsized success for Finnish gaming company Supercell.

By committing to the mantra “trust, don’t control,” Ilkka built Supercell around small, nimble teams. Those autonomous teams, insulated from outside meddling and armed with maximal freedom, create a corporate culture resembling professional sports teams. The organization exists to support high performers who are empowered to focus on their individual role.

This approach has generated startling results. With an extreme commitment to excellence, Supercell has released just five games, ever. All of them have become worldwide top-10 iOS and Android releases, and four have grossed more than $1 billion.

Quote: “We’ve replaced the control with trust. The best days at Supercell, for me, are when I hear about something way later than it has been decided. That proves the model is working.”

GetYourGuide
- Johannes and Tao re-invented travel experiences with the fastest-scaling tour and activities booking platform in the world.

Without a fateful travel mix-up, the global booking platform GetYourGuide might never have existed. It began when Johannes, then a student at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology, arrived early for a holiday in Beijing. He spent days wandering the streets, unsure of how to fill his time and what to see. Once his classmate Tao, a Beijing native, arrived to act as a local guide, the duo were able to get the most out of the experience.

Along the way, they sketched out a vision that became GetYourGuide. Cheap flights and great hotels were easy to book anywhere in the world. But bookings for what Johannes calls the fun parts of a trip — tours, activities, social connection with locals — were scattered, and ripe for disruption.

When they returned to school, the men tapped two other friends in Zurich who, like them, shared a talent for engineering and a love of travel, and got to work. One pivot, one pandemic, and over $1 billion in funding later, GetYourGuide has emerged as a leading global platform for experiences. Travelers have used it to book more than 80 millions tours, allowing people everywhere to experience destinations richly and authentically.

Power of B2B

We are at an inflection point of global tech: Covid-19 pandemic has dramatically accelerated technology adoption in B2B: There is no economic reason for any part of the value chain not to be digitized in the long run if there is potential to streamline and automate processes. From developer tools to vertical-specific applications, we expect software to power all areas of business. Work is increasingly digital, distributed, and getting more complex.

People need better tools to do their jobs and free up time for personal health and self fulfillment. Businesses will thus look more and more like tech companies – the digital world will need new infrastructure, both hardware and software. This time we are in a strong position in Europe to shape the next wave of disruption in B2B: Europe is home to many global industrial world market leaders, most of them built on legacy technology with the potential to be either disrupted or enabled by new digital technologies.
