Sahit Dendekuri

Investor, Growth

Sahit is an investor on Spark's growth team, where he looks for big ideas the future will take for granted, and the singular founders who could make them real. “With every founder, I like to first imagine the most ambitious long-term vision for their company,” he says, “and then figure out what has to be true to get there.”

He grew up in Texas and went to Stanford, where he studied Symbolic Systems and Computer Science. Before joining Spark, he was an investor at Newlands, investing in and evaluating technology companies across their full lifecycle, from the world's largest public companies to startups just getting off the ground.

Outside of work, he enjoys traveling and watching movies.
