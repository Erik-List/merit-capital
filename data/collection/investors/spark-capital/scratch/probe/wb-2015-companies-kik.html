Kik

Founded in 2010, Kik Interactive Inc is the maker of Kik Messenger, the fast, simple, and personal mobile messenger.  Over 50 million people are already using Kik, and Kik continues to grow rapidly with over 200,000 new people joining every day.

Kik is the way people love to connect in mobile, and share the things they care about with the people they care about. Kik Interactive believes the future of smartphone era computing is in the mobile web.  Kik is committed to building a cutting-edge HTML5 platform, making it easy for developers everywhere to build, distribute, and monetize new experiences for the mobile web. Kik Interactive is a privately-held company headquartered in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada.
