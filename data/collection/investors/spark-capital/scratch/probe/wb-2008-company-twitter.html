Twitter

Twitter is a new kind of communication utility inspired by the concept of a status or “away” message. Updates of 140 characters or less are shared in real-time among networks of individuals through Twitter over a variety of existing services around the world. Twitter currently works over Mobile Texting (SMS), Instant Message, Social Networks and other web sites, and hundreds of specialty applications built on an open API. For more information please visit twitter.com.
