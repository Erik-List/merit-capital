# EQT Ventures: investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../../COLLECTION.md` to EQT Ventures (slug
`eqt-ventures`, display name always **EQT Ventures**). The judge cites these
rulings per line. Written at kickoff 2026-09-27 (PRO-163) under the derivation
protocol of COLLECTION.md §1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to
verified facts; no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's), and
**run-time standing rulings** (dated, class-wide, flagged for review).

**The structural fact of this run:** EQT Ventures is not a firm with its own
adviser. It is one strategy of EQT AB (Nasdaq Stockholm), whose funds are all
managed by one Luxembourg AIFM, so adviser identity cannot draw the boundary and
the fund family does (T1, strategy desks, Erik 2026-09-04, which names this
firm). The firm's own record is unusually good at exactly that: every EQT
portfolio page states the fund that holds the company, its entry year and its
exit year, for current and former holdings alike. What it does not state is a
round, so entry stages come from the firm's own announcements (the stories
archive, 172 posts since 2016) and the record.

## Derivations (T1 to T5 applied to verified facts; vetoable, no confirmation needed)

- **D2: EQT Growth is another desk (T1, strategy desks; PRO-163's named
  boundary case, ruled at kickoff as the issue asks, flagged for Erik).**
  Facts: EQT Growth is its own fund family, **EQT Growth (No.1) SCSp**
  (805-6589209998, $2,030,061,600; CIK 1866858) and **(No.2)** (805-5666351993,
  $165,941,725; CIK 1866869) with co-investment vehicles (A), (B), (C), (K) and
  EQT Growth Network SCSp, under its own general partner, **EQT Growth (General
  Partner) S.à r.l.**; vintage 2021, EUR 2.4B total commitments (closed
  2022-09-12). The firm's own words at that close: "EQT Ventures and EQT
  Growth are both committed to investing in high growth private tech
  companies; they are two separate business divisions, with separate funds and
  advisory teams", with initial tickets of EUR 1–50M against EUR 50–200M. The
  site keeps them apart today: its own strategy pages (Ventures "2-50M EUR";
  Growth "50–250M EUR", 15 companies), its `business-line` people filter, and
  each portfolio page's Fund field. What moved in 2025: Carolina Brochado is
  "Partner, Head of EQT Growth US and Head of EQT Ventures", Victor Englesson
  appears on both team lists, EQT AB's fund table groups them as "EQT Ventures &
  Growth", and the stories blog carries Growth posts. Leadership converged; the
  funds did not.
  Test: T1's strategy-desk addendum partitions one adviser's funds by strategy
  and resolves the desk **per vehicle**, never by the brand that announced the
  deal. Adviser identity is silent here (one AIFM for everything), so the
  vehicle decides, and an EQT Growth fund is not an EQT Ventures fund. The
  Accel, Lightspeed and Thrive growth sleeves that were ruled IN were run by the
  same partnership under the same brand; EQT Growth was raised and staffed as a
  separate division. **Outcome: `growth` is `other_desk`.** Its 16 site rows
  enter the base build and leave it into `excluded.json` (reason `other_desk`,
  lineage `growth`), preserved and counted. **Where an EQT Ventures fund
  entered and EQT Growth followed, the row is a member through the Ventures
  entry** (PRO-163's instruction; Wolt: "EQT Growth, EQT Ventures I", entry
  2016), and its entry is the Ventures fund's first check. The profile is sliced
  the same way: EQT Growth's gross assets never enter `aum`.
  Why this is a delta, not only a derivation: the growth-sleeve precedents
  point IN and the vehicle test points OUT, so the facts satisfy two readings.
  The ruling moves 16 rows (Vinted, Mollie, Mambu, Epidemic Sound, Harvey,
  Lovable, Mews, ManyPets, Wellhub, ReliaQuest, Perk, Ardoq, CluePoints,
  GotPhoto, IntegrityNext, Dwelly) and flips mechanically: remove `growth` from
  `config.desks.partition` and rebuild.

  EQT Private Equity (EQT I to X, Mid Market, Expansion Capital, Opportunity,
  country funds), BPEA / Private Capital Asia, EQT Infrastructure and Active
  Core, EQT Life Sciences (the LSP funds, a separate firm acquired in 2022 whose

  another layer surfaces can be checked against the fund that actually held it.
  **The Scaleup Europe Fund** (EUR 5B target, EQT selected as manager by the
  European Commission and founding investors, 2026-05-18; co-heads Ted Persson
  and Victor Englesson; Series B onward) is its own fund family under a public
  mandate: `scaleup_europe`, `other_desk`. Its first deals (Lovable, Mistral AI,
  Tandem Health, EUCLYD, Fever, and a co-lead of The Exploration Company's
  Series C) are not rows of this base, except where an EQT Ventures fund
  entered first (The Exploration Company, EQT Ventures III, 2023).

- **D4: Fund money (T2).** OUT: partners' personal angel checks, including
  before they joined; positions of the Mentor vehicles only where no EQT
  Ventures fund held the company (none expected). The firm's "EQT Ventures
  Mentor" network and scouts are not a fund. IN: every company an EQT Ventures
  fund held, whatever the program.

- **D5: Venture entry (T3).** EQT Ventures makes minority venture rounds, as
  lead ("As early-stage lead investors", the strategy page). Control positions
  and public-market purchases are OUT and not expected. **A company later bought
  by another EQT strategy** (Banking Circle: "EQT VIII, EQT Ventures I", entry
  2018) is a member only through an EQT Ventures venture round, judged on its
  own facts; the buyout is `other_desk` and never the entry.

- **D6: Person or firm (T4).** A Form D or register seat is dated against the
  person's firm at filing time. The team moves inside EQT (Growth, the Scaleup
  Europe Fund, private equity): a seat held by a Ventures partner for an EQT
  Growth or Scaleup Europe position is that fund's, never this base's.

- **D7: Operating company (T5).** Standard. The EQT Foundation's grants, EQT
  AB's own subsidiaries (Motherbrain) and fund vehicles are not rows.

- **D8: Registers (§5 table).** Home registers give `status` (Bolagsverket for
  Sweden, Companies House for the UK, the Handelsregister for Germany, PRH for
  Finland, the Brønnøysund registers for Norway, CVR for Denmark). Membership
  layers only where §5 says a register answers the direction: the UK share
  register (Balderton's R3) per company, and Norway's and Denmark's
  owner-searchable registers for a forward read of EQT Ventures' holdings.

- **D9: The site's fields (evidence rules).** The EQT portfolio page is the
  firm's own listing: membership evidence (`site_listing`) for every company
  whose Fund field names an EQT Ventures fund. Its **Entry** field is a bare
  year: it anchors `first_seen` and bounds entry recency and never mints a
  stage (COLLECTION.md §6). Its **Exit** field is the firm's own statement that
  the funds left the company, not a status: the status still comes from the
  record (a sale, a listing, a shutdown, a secondary sale by the fund). The
  stories archive is class 1 for the round a post **names**, and an entry only
  where the post says the fund entered (Menlo's announcement lesson).

## Run-time standing rulings

(Dated, class-wide, flagged for review.)

- **R1: A purchase into a company already listed is not a venture entry
  (2026-09-27; T3 and the public-purchase rule).** BIMobject AB had traded on
  Nasdaq First North since 2012 when EQT Ventures put EUR 23.4M into it in July
  2018 ("You just invested a big ticket in a public company?", the firm's own
  post, 2018-07-18). The firm listed it from 2018 to 2025, but the position
  began as a public-market purchase, so the row is `not_member`. Flagged for
  Erik because the firm's own listing says otherwise.
- **R2: A position that arrives by demerger is not a member (ruled by Erik,
  2026-09-27, reversing the run's first reading).** Docly was split out of Min
  Doktor in August 2018, so the fund came to hold it without buying into a
  round; the firm listed it 2019 to 2022 and "declined to make a follow-up
  investment" (Sifted). Stock received through a portfolio company's corporate
  event is the class of the sale-consideration ruling (2026-09-22): the
  investment is Min Doktor, which keeps its row, and Docly is `not_member`.
- **R3: A later round the record names a seed does not graduate a Seed row
  (2026-09-27; applies Erik's named-round ruling of 2026-09-17).** The round's
  name is the fact the column asks about, and the 2x / $5M step-up decides
  only rounds with no name. Kive's Oct 2022 $7M round (Tech.eu: "Heartcore
  leads $7 million seed round") and Supernormal's Jan 2023 $10M round
  (Business Insider: "Raises $10m Seed Round") therefore leave both rows
  `not_graduated`; a16z reads Payrails' seed extension the same way. Creandum's
  Kive row reads the same round as graduated by the step-up: the two books
  differ, raised for Erik.
- **R4: A current portfolio page that names no fund keeps the row a member
  when the firm's own announcement is the Ventures team's (2026-09-27; D9).**
  Ineffable Intelligence and Starcloud (both entered 2026) carry no Fund field
  on eqtgroup.com. Ineffable's seed was announced in a post by the EQT
  Ventures team (2026-04-27), before the Scaleup Europe Fund existed;
  Starcloud's $170M Series A was "led by Benchmark and EQT Ventures"
  (Wikipedia, citing SpaceNews, 2026-03-30). Member, flagged for Erik: the
  pages will name the fund once EQT updates them, and a Growth or Scaleup
  Europe label then moves the row out under D2 or D3.
