Eric Mosley. Globoforce

Follow:
Website: http://www.globoforce.com/

Globoforce is the world’s leading provider of strategic, on-demand employee recognition programs for Global 2000 companies.  Globoforce transforms the way companies engage, motivate and empower their workforces across the globe, making recognition meaningful to every employee everywhere in the world by enabling the rapid delivery of daily thank-you moments that are culturally relevant and personally desirable. Globoforce’s strategic approach to recognition proves the value the program delivers to a company’s bottom line through more engaged, more productive employees who drive company performance. 

Eric Mosley

As co-founder and CEO of Globoforce, Eric has been directing the path of Globoforce as the innovator in the strategic employee recognition industry since the company’s beginning. His vision to raise employee recognition from a tactical, unmeasured, and under-valued effort to a global strategic program with clear measures for performance and success is now being realized in some of the world’s largest and most complex organizations.
