Xavier Flinois. Clinical Solutions

Follow:
Website: www.csdss.com

Clinical Solutions is the leading provider of healthcare software solutions and services for clinical decision support, informatics, nurse triage and assessment.

Operating from offices in the UK, US and Australia, customers include NHS Direct in England and Wales, NHS 24 in Scotland, many of the Walk in Centres across England, and also customers in Portugal, Norway, Australia, New Zealand and the USA.

Xavier Flinois

Xavier Flinois joined Clinical Solutions in October 2009 as Chief Executive Officer from consulting and investment boutique Houat Partners. Xavier has over twenty years experience in technology and health IT projects around the world as a former CEO of SchlumbergerSema and a member of the Management Board of AtosOrigin, in charge of the UK, Americas and Asia.

Under Xavier's leadership, AtosOrigin became a leading player in the health Industry in the UK, both as an IT supplier to the NHS and as the leading provider of medical services to the occupational healthcare and the benefit assessment markets.

After AtosOrigin, Xavier founded Houat Partners LLP, an advisory firm based in London, providing origination and co-investment services to hedge and private equity funds in the technology, energy and business services sectors.

Xavier is married with four children. While he enjoys history and visiting old cities around the world, his main extra professional activity outside his family is competitive sailing.
