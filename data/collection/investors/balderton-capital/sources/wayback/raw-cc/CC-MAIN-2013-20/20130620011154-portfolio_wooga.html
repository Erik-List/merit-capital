Jens Begemann. Wooga

Follow:
Website: www.wooga.com

Wooga was founded in January 2009 and develops social games for networks like Facebook and social mobile games for the App Store.

Wooga is among the Top 3 social game developers worldwide and is well known for its successful games Brain Buddies, Bubble Island, Monster World and Diamond Dash and Magic Land.

Being based in the heart of Berlin, an international team from 28 nations develops high-quality, emotional social games that continue to raise the bar.

Jens Begemann

Jens has always been enthusiastic about people, play and technology. Running a company with skilled people from all over the world and developing a high quality gaming experience in a social environment is like living a dream for him. Jens founded Wooga in 2009.

Wooga is the 3rd largest developer of social games worldwide and the leading developer based in Europe. Wooga employs a team of over 150 people from 28 nations and is located in the heart of Berlin.

Before Wooga Jens was Chief Product Officer & Member of the Management Board at Jamba (Jamster). For seven years he helped the company become the world's leading provider of mobile entertainment products. He managed a team of 130 people and was responsible for content acquisition & creation and user experience.
