Ivan Retzignac & Andrew Bucher. MedicAnimal

Follow:
Website: http://www.medicanimal.com/

MedicAnimal.com is an online retailer of pet supplies, including food, supplements, medicines and accessories with a particular focus on veterinary diets, prescription and non-prescription medicines and premium-quality products. MedicAnimal is registered both as a veterinary dispensary as well as a pharmacy in the UK and offers clients free advice from its in-house team of veterinarians and pharmacists via live chat, email or phone. The site also publishes news and advice related to general animal care and health issues. MedicAnimal.com is based in London, UK and is already a reference in the pet and veterinary retail world throughout Europe.

Ivan Retzignac & Andrew Bucher

Ivan Retzignac previously worked in finance in a range of areas, including equity research, venture capital, and most recently on the commodities Sales & Trading desk of Goldman Sachs in London which he left in 2007 to found MedicAnimal. Ivan holds a BA from Tufts University and an MBA from Columbia Business School. 

Andrew Bucher MRCVS co-founded MedicAnimal in 2007, prior to which he spent 6 years as a practising veterinary surgeon in the UK and Australia. Before becoming a veterinarian, Andrew worked as territory manager for Roche Pharmaceuticals. Andrew holds a BSc in Biotechnology from the University of Reading, as well as a Doctorate in Veterinary Medicine from the University of Queensland, Australia.
