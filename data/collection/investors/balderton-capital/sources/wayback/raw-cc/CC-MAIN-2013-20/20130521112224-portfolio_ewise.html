Alexandre Gonthier. eWise

Follow:
Website: www.ewise.com

eWise is a global provider of online payments and financial management solutions.
 
eWise aims to transform the way people pay online by establishing online banking ePayment networks that allow customers to pay direct from their bank account without needing to disclose personal financial details, and avoiding the high fees associated with credit card transactions.
 
In addition, eWise’s online Personal Financial Management system enables customers to see all their bank accounts and finances in one place.

Alexandre Gonthier

Alex is a serial entrepreneur and noted innovator in the payments space. He co-founded iPIN in San Francisco in 1997, a leading online and mobile payments platform, which became Valista in 2004 and is now part of Aepona, generating millions of transactions per day for banks and telecom operators such as Wells Fargo, HSBC, AT&T, Vodafone, BT and France Telecom.  Alex was also the co-founding CTO of San Francisco and London-based mobile payments company Paymo in 2007, which has since merged with Boku, the most ubiquitous mobile payment method globally serving companies like Facebook, Electronic Arts and Zynga.  Until late 2011, Alex was online and payment sector advisor to global private equity firm 3i and venture capital firm Wellington Partners in London.  As venture partner for Wellington Partners, Alex led the latest financing round in eWise and became so inspired with the company’s vision that he moved back to Silicon Valley to join as CEO.  Alex holds a Masters in Communications Systems from EPFL, the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne.
