Mathieu Gosselin. Psykosoft

Follow:
Website: http://www.psykosoft.net/

We believe in originality; we believe in personality; we believe in emotions.  All these things are not usually associated with software. But software is just a means to an end. We want to build software made for human beings, not robots. Software which adapts to you, not the way around. Of course we use some uber technology and the most fantastic minds to do that. But leave the rocket science to us and the fun to you. We intend to push the boundaries of what has been done before. We want to change the world. Other software companies would say we are crazy. Indeed we are.

Mathieu Gosselin

Mathieu is the crazy mind behind Psykosoft. He used to be a creative technologist contracting for brands like Nike, Ford, Nokia, Landrover, Burberry... before deciding he'd rather spend time doing what he really loved and created Psykopaint.
