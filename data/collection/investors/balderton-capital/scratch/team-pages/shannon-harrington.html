Shannon Harrington

Head of Portfolio Talent

Overview

Shannon joined Balderton in December 2023 and is the Head of Portfolio Talent and Network. She works alongside Balderton’s portfolio as a consultative partner on strategic hiring, people challenges, and the thorny cultural questions.

Before Balderton, Shannon spent time in venture talent at Bain Capital Ventures and Greylock Partners, and before that was an early employee at TellApart (acquired by Twitter for $500M), helping scale the team from 20 people upward. She’s driven technical recruiting for companies in their formative phases, including Snowflake, Anduril, Clumio, Verkada, Sigma Computing, and Abnormal Security.

Shannon holds a BA from Stanford University, where she also competed on the Women’s Varsity Squash team. Outside of work she’s an avid racket sport player, psychology book reader, and board game enthusiast.
