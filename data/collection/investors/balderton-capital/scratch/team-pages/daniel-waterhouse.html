Daniel Waterhouse

Partner

Overview

Daniel joined Balderton as a Partner in 2013 and has a broad focus across many areas but has recently invested in the areas of fusion, AI-powered health, enterprise GTM software and consumer platforms.

Daniel currently sits on the boards of and/or works with Revolut, Zoe, Fuse, Proxima Fusion, Attio, Voize, vivenu, Merama, Sylvera, Workable and others.

He also worked on Balderton’s Liquidity Fund and led this Fund’s investments in (amongst others) Darktrace (IPO), Truecaller (IPO), Housecall Pro, Lendable and McMakler.

Prior to Balderton, Daniel spent five years as a partner at Wellington Partners and worked on investments, including Spotify (IPO on NYSE), Hailo (FREE NOW), Qype (Yelp), Readmill (Dropbox), and Bookatable (Michelin).

Before Wellington, Daniel was a sector partner at 3i where he worked on all of their venture and private equity investments in the internet sector in North America and Europe. Before that, he was at Yahoo! for six years from 1999 in the European corporate development team.

Daniel has a PhD and MA in applied mathematics from Oxford University and a Chartered Diploma in Accounting and Finance.
