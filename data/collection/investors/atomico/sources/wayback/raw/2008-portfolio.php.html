DECA is the world's premier digital entertainment company.

DECA develops, finances, produces, distributes, and monetizes video-centric social properties. DECA are dedicated to bridging the gap between television and the Internet. Their properties reach connected audiences on all the platforms and devices they use, with high-quality, relevant content. Their commitment to talent and creative freedom produces authentic and engaging video and social media. The production value and editorial caliber of DECA properties have attracted some of the world's biggest sponsors, including Visa, BMW, Adobe, IBM, Verizon, Bank of America, Sandisk, and Dell.

Headquartered in Santa Monica, CA, the company was founded in 2007 by Michael Wayne and Chris Kimbell. DECA is funded by Mayfield, Atomico, and General Catalyst Partners.
