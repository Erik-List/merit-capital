    Capital is a commodity. Experience is not.

    Atomico is a different sort of venture capital firm. The way we operate is not typical. But it is carefully designed to help the most gamechanging entrepreneurs build technology to rewire the world, better.

    Why Atomico is different

    Our areas of interest.

    Our overarching thesis is that technology will transform all sectors of our world and that the biggest transformations are yet to come. Every few years there are new frontiers opening for innovation. Founders are the gamechangers that we take our cue from. As a thematic investor, these are just some of the current areas we are focusing on.

    • With the creation of business applications that empower workers & enhance productivity, the digital transformation of every industry is under way.

      Digital Work

    • Enterprise AI and automation platforms are becoming the brains of tomorrow’s modern enterprises.

      Enterprise AI & Automation

    • Progressive founders are developing the foundational infrastructure that will be the ‘connective tissue’ of every modern business.

      Internet Infrastructure & Security

    • Brilliant founders are re-imagining the services and underlying infrastructure that power financial systems and define how money flows.

      Future Finance

    • A mobility revolution is underway. The services and infrastructure that move people and goods, and let us travel the world, are undergoing dynamic innovation.

      Mobility

    • Making industries such as manufacturing, logistics and construction more efficient is a global challenge at a critical time for humanity.

      Industrial Automation

    • Today’s (and tomorrow’s) tech entrepreneurs are harnessing scientific breakthroughs and emerging forms of tech to rewire entire industries and solve global challenges.

      Frontier Tech

    • Extraordinary entrepreneurs are the world’s best bet in tackling dire global issues like the climate crisis and the need for decarbonisation.

      Planet Positive

    • Consumer companies are devising ways to make our patterns of commerce and consumption more engaging, more sustainable, and more in line with global sentiment.

      Engaged Consumer

    • Creative entrepreneurs are reinventing the way societies produce, distribute, and consume food.

      Future of Food

    • Entrepreneurs in this field are dedicated to enabling individuals and social communities to lead happier, more connected and fulfilled lives.

      Digital Life & Play

    • Founders are using cutting-edge tech to focus on complete biological pictures & consumer-driven care, sparking a foundational shift in how societies view healthcare.

      Improving Health

    • It’s something everyone in the VC world should care about. The companies in this space are empowering individuals to reach their full potential.

      Empowered Individuals
