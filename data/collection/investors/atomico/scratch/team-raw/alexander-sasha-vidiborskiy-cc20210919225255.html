Alexander (Sasha) Vidiborskiy
Principal

Alexander (Sasha) Vidiborskiy is a Principal at Atomico based in London. He is a quantum physicist by background and specialises in enterprise software including developer tools, cloud infrastructure, and AI/ML applications among others. He has worked on Atomico’s investments in arculus and Qatalog.

Before joining Atomico, Sasha was a Senior Associate at Runa Capital where he worked with entrepreneurs from Nginx, Keymetrics / PM2, Yoopies, Cloudlinux and many others. Prior to that he ran Business Operations at Bitcalm, a 1-click backup and recovery SaaS for SMEs. Sasha started his career as a Research Associate at a theoretical physics lab where he built a first-in-class tunable superconducting metamaterials.

Sasha holds BSc in Microelectronics and MSc in Theoretical Physics from MISIS, MSc in Innovation Studies from Skoltech in collaboration with MIT, and an MBA from Harvard Business School.

In his spare he enjoys reading sci-fi, snowboarding or playing ping-pong. Sasha can read Dostoevsky in Russian and explain what he did last summer in Romanian.
