Founded by Cesar Carvalho

Gympass

Gympass partners with employers to grant staff access to health, wellbeing and fitness solutions. Employers pay a flat fee for the platform which then allows their employees to choose from several wellbeing plans that give them access to myriad in-person gyms and studios, and a directory of health apps, such as Calm.

With over 50,000 fitness partners, 1,300 on-demand classes, 2,000 hours of meditation, weekly 1:1 therapy sessions, and hundreds of personal trainers, Gympass supports every wellbeing journey. They partner with best-in-class wellbeing providers in multiple markets across North America, Latin America, and Europe.
