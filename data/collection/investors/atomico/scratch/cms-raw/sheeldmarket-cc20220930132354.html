SheeldMarket

SheeldMarket provides institutional investors with access to the digital assets marketplace. SheeldMarket is an EU-registered broker that prevents investors from needing to navigate a patchwork of rules and regulations that breed uncertainty.

SheeldMarket provides institutional investors with access to the digital assets marketplace through its smart order routing algorithms, which lead the industry for capacity, flexibility and execution in the cryptocurrency market.

SheeldMarket is an EU-registered broker that prevents investors from needing to navigate a patchwork of rules and regulations that breed uncertainty.

SheeldMarket was founded in 2019 by former blockchain engineer Oliver Yates, former Credit Suisse exec Jacques Lolieux, crypto mining engineer Arnaud Carrere, and former Bitcoin Lightning engineer Simon Douyer. The SheeldMarket team have made rapid advances in rolling out their institutional cryptocurrency trading platform to a wide range of customers, while its best-in-class algorithm is already trusted by dozens of professional investors to optimise their investments.
