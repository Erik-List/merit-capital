Jawbone

Jawbone was a world leader in consumer technology and wearable products. Founded in 1997, and headquartered in San Francisco, Jawbone became an early wearable unicorn and made personal technology comfortable, usable, and beautiful. 

The flagship product, the award-winning Jawbone Bluetooth headset with NoiseAssassin™ technology set a new standard for mobile voice quality. The company later added wearable fitness trackers - in the form of flexible, water-proof rubber coated bracelets - that allowed users to track their sleep, eating habits, and daily activity including steps taken and calories burned.
