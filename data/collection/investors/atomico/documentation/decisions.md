# Atomico — investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../../COLLECTION.md` to Atomico (slug
`atomico`, display name always **Atomico**). The judge cites these rulings per
line. Written at kickoff (PRO-79, 2026-09-26) under the derivation protocol of
COLLECTION.md §1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to verified facts;
no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's, confirmed before
kickoff), and **run-time standing rulings** (dated, class-wide, flagged for
review). PRO-79's Phase 0 was never drafted; this file is its derivation, and
the derivations are posted on the issue so Erik can veto any one.

**The structural fact of this run:** Atomico is a London firm whose portfolio
is mostly European and whose companies file nothing with the SEC, while its
own fund side is visible there (nine fund filers, two exempt reporting
advisers with Section 7.B tables, and fund-level ownership filings on the US
listings: Hinge Health's Form 3/4 and 13G, Klarna's F-1, Lilium's F-4,
Compass's S-1). What replaces the portfolio-side Form D is (1) the firm's own
record read in depth — seven archived listing eras 2008–2026, one of which
(2011) files every company under the fund that held it, and a current CMS that
names the investment year, the lead partner and a Venture / Growth label per
company — (2) the Getro job board, and (3) the new UK layer this run builds:
the Companies House **officer graph** (every UK directorship of the firm's
people, dated, with the service address on the appointment) and the
register's company status.

- **D2 — The founders' own companies and personal positions (T2 fund money,

  Joltid and Rdio before and beside the firm, and Zennström holds a family
  office (Hanover Limited, Aston House, London N3) and a philanthropy. A

  position in it** (fund money), never because a founder built or owned it.
  Verified facts that decide two named cases: (a) **Skype** — Skype's own S-1

  Skype holder, so a Skype listing on the firm's site is the founders'

- **D9 — The UK officer graph is corroboration with a date, not membership
  alone (§1 evidence rules, T4).** A director appointment of an Atomico person
  inside their tenure is the register's analogue of a Form D related-person
  seat: it corroborates a listed row and dates its entry, and it is
  **affirmative** for an unlisted company only when the appointment's service
  address is an Atomico office (Hanover House 14 Hanover Square, 7 Savoy
  Court, 50 New Bond Street, 29-35 Rathbone Street) or the seat passes
  between two Atomico people (LendInvest: Ljungman 2016-03 → 2019-11, Barnes
  2019-11 → 2021-07), and the company resolves to a real operating business
  (the a16z director-of-record narrowing). A seat with neither is a lead for
  the recall probe, never a verdict. Seats in the person's own vehicles
  (family office, philanthropy, sailing companies, the firm's own entities)
  are not rows.

- **D10 — The firm's own per-company fields.** The current CMS publishes, per
  company, an **investment year**, an **investment lead**, a **stage** of
  `Venture` or `Growth` and a **status** of `Current` or `Exited`. The year
  is an anchor (it may set `first_seen` and `entry_date` for the censor
  clock), never a round. `Growth` collapses onto one taxonomy stage and is
  eligible only if it is the firm's claim about the round it entered rather
  than the sleeve that holds the position; `Venture` straddles Seed, Series A
  and B and is an anchor like a bare year. Both are scored against documented
  rounds before either labels a row (COLLECTION.md §5, PRO-135): the field
  enters `site_api.entry_stage_fields` only if the score clears. The lead
  partner is the attribution map for the officer graph and any name-match
  cluster.

- **D11 — The job board is a first-party list surface** (Thrive ruling 8,
  Cherry D11): careers.atomico.com (Getro, the firm's own subdomain) lists 68
  companies on 2026-09-26; a board listing is `site_listing` evidence with the
  standing caveats (it under-lists exits and dead companies; the vendor's
  stage field is never a label).

## Run-time standing rulings (dated, class-wide, flagged for Erik's review)

12. **Skype re-derived under T3's control rule, same outcome** (agent,
    2026-09-26; a clean flip under §1, reported, not escalated). D2(a) was
    derived on the S-1 alone. The firm's own CMS page (Common Crawl capture
    CC-MAIN-2023-23) dates Skype "Investment year 2009, Stage Growth, Status
    Exited", so the facts changed: the firm claims a 2009 position. The only
    2009 transaction was the November 2009 purchase of 65% of Skype from eBay
    by the Silver Lake-led consortium, with the founders' Joltid as a party:
    a co-investor group acquiring control, which T3 (Erik, 2026-09-04) puts
    OUT whatever vehicle took part. The S-1 still shows no Atomico vehicle
    among the holders and Skype committing $10M INTO Atomico Ventures II. So
    Skype is `not_member` under T3 (control), with T4 (the founders' personal
    Joltid stake) as the second ground; the Menlo signature does not apply,
    because the page carries a date and a stage. Flagged: Skype is the firm's
    founding story, and its absence from the track record should be seen.
13. **A site-listed acquirer that holds a portfolio company's successor
    position is consideration** (agent, 2026-09-26; D6 applied to the firm's
    own site, §1 Erik 2026-09-22): **Free Now** is listed with "Investment
    year 2011", Hailo's year, and "Free Now is part of the mobility joint
    venture" that bought Hailo in 2016; the Free Now stake is Hailo's sale
    consideration. `not_member`, Hailo stays the member (acquired by mytaxi
    2016). The same reading will be applied to **Pharmacy2U** (Chemist
    Direct's merger partner: Atomico named as a Pharmacy2U holder only from the
    August 2016 statement, after the merger) if it surfaces as a row.
14. **Telegram is a token-presale position: a member with an undetermined
    round** (agent, 2026-09-26; T5 token rule and the §6 token-funded ruling,
    Erik 2026-09-19): the firm's own page gives "Investment year 2018, Stage
    Growth, Status Exited". Telegram's only 2018 financing was the private sale
    of Gram tokens for the TON network, a token that maps to its issuing
    organisation (Telegram), so it is IN; there is no priced round, so
    `entry_stage` and `seed_to_a_graduation` read `undetermined`, flagged
    `token_funded`, and the row keeps its band and status. Flagged because the
    presale was unwound after the SEC's 2020 injunction (Telegram returned most
    of the money), which no standing rule addresses; a reader may reasonably
    ask whether a refunded presale is a position at all.
15. **SUPERSEDED 2026-09-26 by Erik's T2 rule (6a7165e): Wolt is `not_member`,
    a founder's angel check with no fund shown.** Original reading: **Wolt stays a member on the firm's own listing, flagged as possibly a
    personal position** (agent, 2026-09-26; COLLECTION.md §1 evidence

16. **A job-board listing nothing corroborates stays a member, flagged**
    (agent, 2026-09-26; D11). Ashby (recruiting software, San Francisco) is on

    row.
17. **The UK register is filing-grade membership evidence** (agent,
    2026-09-26; §1 "a filing by a fund vehicle suffices alone"). A Companies
    House annual return or confirmation statement listing an Atomico vehicle
    as a shareholder is a statutory filing by the company, the UK
    counterpart of a fund vehicle on a Form D. It admitted one company no
    other layer saw: **VisualDNA** (Imagini Holdings Ltd, 05730835), whose
    2008 and 2009 returns list Atomico Investment Holdings Ltd. Three more
    firm entities named on the register join D1's list: Atomico Investment
    Holdings Ltd (BVI; it also operated the 2008 site), Atomico Management
    Limited (Zattikka's 2009 return) and Atomico IV (Guernsey) L.P. A
    director seat alone is not evidence (Rabbit Hole Labs, Ben Blume from
    January 2026, allotments in February 2026 but no vehicle named yet: a lead
    for the next run). A register share class ("A PREFERRED", "SERIES SEED")
    is a candidate round word, scored 16/17 on the book's documented rows,
    below the 30-row bar, so it is recorded in notes and never labels a row.
