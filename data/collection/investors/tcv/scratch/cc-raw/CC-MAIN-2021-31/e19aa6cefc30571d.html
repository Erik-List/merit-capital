Brendon Piccinni

Associate

Background

Brendon joined TCV in 2021 as an Associate in the Menlo Park office, where he focuses on investments in the consumer internet sector.

Prior to TCV, Brendon worked at RLH Equity, an LA-based growth fund focused on technology and high-intellect services. He also spent two years at Perella Weinberg Partners in New York, focused on TMT.

Interests

Brendon was born and raised in the Boston area, where he grew up lobstering and boat building. He still maintains a lobstering license in Massachusetts. When not in the office or on the water, you’ll find him playing ice hockey, biking, or trying new restaurants.

Education

Brendon received a B.S. with Honors from Cornell University.
