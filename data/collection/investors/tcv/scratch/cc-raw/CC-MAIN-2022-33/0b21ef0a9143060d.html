Efforts and courage are not enough without purpose and direction.

John F. Kennedy

Erez Elisha

General Partner
Chief Financial Officer

Background

Erez joined TCV in 2019, bringing more than 20 years of experience of working with global financial institutions. Erez leads the firm’s global finance, treasury, valuation, tax, and corporate development functions. He also oversees the firm’s IT function.

Prior to TCV, Erez was an Executive Managing Director and Chief Accounting Officer for Sculptor Capital Management, where he managed the Public Entity, Corporate Finance, and Accounting teams. He previously held VP of Finance roles in the Asset Management Divisions at Morgan Stanley and J.P. Morgan. Erez started his career at PricewaterhouseCoopers.

Interests

Erez enjoys a broad range of music and has been playing the piano since childhood. He loves spending time with his family and can be found on lacrosse fields on weekends, cheering for his children’s teams. Erez has also been an active supporter and volunteer of the Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center (MSKCC), which was established in 1945 with the vision of harnessing the latest research techniques to conquer cancer.

Education

Erez holds a B.S. in Accounting from Florida Atlantic University and an M.B.A. from the University of Miami. Erez is a C.P.A. certified in the State of Georgia.
