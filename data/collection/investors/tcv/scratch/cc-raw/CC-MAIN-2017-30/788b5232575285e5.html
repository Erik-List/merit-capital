Fortune favors the bold.

Jessica Lee

Investor Relations Senior Analyst

Background
Jessica focuses on TCV’s engagement with its Limited Partners as well as the strategy and execution around capital raising.

Prior to joining TCV, Jessica worked in the Leveraged Finance group at Wells Fargo Securities. Her experience includes debt financing transactions for companies across a number of industries.

Interests
Born and raised in the Bay Area, Jessica lived several years in Seoul, South Korea, where she attended international school and picked up figure skating, which soon became her life. She spent her childhood training to win the Winter Olympics and in the process became a nationally and internationally ranked competitive figure skater. She now spends her free time watching ballet, dancing (terribly), painting (also terribly), and playing with her mini Goldendoodle.

Education
Jessica received her B.A. in Political Science from University of California, Los Angeles.

Location
Palo Alto
