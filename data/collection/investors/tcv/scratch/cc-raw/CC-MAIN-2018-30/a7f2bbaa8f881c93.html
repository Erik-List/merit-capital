Zachary Riedy

Associate

Background

Zach joined TCV in 2018 as an Associate in the New York office and focuses on investments in the enterprise software sector. Zach supports TCV’s investments in Actifio, Cradlepoint, ExtraHop, Fuze, and Twilio.

Prior to joining TCV, Zach was an Associate at North Bridge Growth Equity (now Guidepost Growth Equity), where he focused on investments across the tech industry. Zach began his career as an Analyst in the Technology Investment Banking Group at William Blair & Company.

Interests
Zach was born and raised in East Tennessee and has since lived in Columbus, Chicago, Boston, and New York. Outside of work, he enjoys traveling the world (his favorite destination to date is the Algarve, Portugal), rooting for the Ohio State Buckeyes, skiing, running, and practicing guitar. He also finds time to work with non-profit organizations supporting education and community development.

Education
Zach received a B.S.B.A. in Finance and Economics with honors from the Ohio State University and studied at the London School of Economics.
