Background
Nari joined TCV in 2006 and focuses on investments in the fintech, healthcare IT, software, and tech-enabled services sectors. Current investments include Avalara, AxiomSL, HireVue, OneSource Virtual, Payoneer, and Varsity Tutors. Previous investments include EmbanetCompass (acquired by Pearson) and Merkle (acquired by Dentsu).

Prior to TCV, Nari was with McKinsey & Company in the San Francisco office, where he focused on assisting clients in the software, storage, and semiconductor sectors.

Interests

Education
Nari received his M.S. and B.S. in Management Science & Engineering (MS&E) from Stanford University’s School of Engineering.
