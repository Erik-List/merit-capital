    Jay Hoag

    Founding General Partner, Executive Committee

    Location

    Menlo Park

    Year Joined

    1995

    Background

    Jay has been a technology investor for over 40 years. He and Rick Kimball co-founded TCV in 1995. Since that time, TCV has raised ~$24B in capital and emerged as a leading provider of growth capital to technology companies. Jay is chair of TCV’s Investment Committee and is also a member of TCV’s Executive Committee, which oversees general management of the firm and its funds. Prior to TCV, Jay was a Managing Director at Chancellor Capital Management, where he spent more than 12 years as a technology-focused venture capitalist and fund manager. He has been involved in a large number of technology investments, including Actuate Software (acquired by OpenText), Airbnb (NASDAQ: ABNB), Ariba (acquired by SAP), Altiris (acquired by Symantec), Anthropic, BlueCoat Systems (formerly CacheFlow), C|NET, eHarmony, Electronic Arts (NASDAQ: EA), Encompass (acquired by Yahoo!), EXE Technologies (acquired by SSA Global), Expedia (NASDAQ: EXPE), Fandango (acquired by Comcast), Groupon (NASDAQ: GRPN), Grow Therapy, LinkedIn, Meta (formerly Facebook) (NASDAQ: META), Netflix (NASDAQ: NFLX), ONYX Software (NASDAQ: ONXS), Peloton (NASDAQ: PTON), Prodege (parent company of Swagbucks & acquired by a private equity firm), RealNetworks (NASDAQ: RNWK), Rover (NASDAQ: ROVR), Sportradar (NASDAQ: SRAD), Spotify (NYSE: SPOT), SpringStreet (acquired by Homestore.com), Strava, TechTarget (NASDAQ: TTGT), Toast (NYSE: TOST), TripAdvisor (NASDAQ: TRIP), Vacationspot.com (acquired by Expedia), Viant (acquired by iXL), and Zillow (NASDAQ: ZG).

    Interests

    Jay is on the Investment Advisory Committee at the University of Michigan, the Board of Trustees of Northwestern University, and is a Trustee Emeritus at Vanderbilt University.

    Education

    Jay received a B.A. in Economics and Political Science from Northwestern University and an M.B.A. from the University of Michigan.
