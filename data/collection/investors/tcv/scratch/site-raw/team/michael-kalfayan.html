    Michael Kalfayan

    General Partner

    Location

    London

    Year Joined

    2014

    Background

    Mike has over a decade of experience investing in European technology companies, and focuses on investments in fintech, consumer, enterprise software, and AI. He is currently involved with investments in Adevinta, Allica Bank, Believe, Flix, LayerX, Mambu, Miro, Qonto, Redis, Revolut, Sportradar (NASDAQ: SRAD), TourRadar, and Trade Republic. He was formerly involved with investments in Siteminder and Brillen. Mike rejoined TCV in 2017 from SiteMinder, where he was Head of Business Operations. Prior to TCV, he was an investor with Summit Partners, where he focused on sourcing, evaluating, and executing investments across the technology and healthcare sectors.

    Interests

    Mike was born and raised in the United Kingdom, and also lived/worked in the U.S., Europe, and Australia. Mike is a passionate tennis player and captained the Harvard Men’s Varsity Tennis Team during his undergraduate days. He enjoys most sports, reading, and spending time with family.

    Education

    Mike graduated cum laude from Harvard University, where he received an A.B. in Social Studies. He also holds an L.L.B. from the University of Law.
