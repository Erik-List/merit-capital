    Anita Pramoda

    Executive Advisor

    Location

    Menlo Park

    Year Joined

    2014

    Background

    Anita is CEO of Owned Outcomes (O2), a software company that helps participating organizations win at value based care and transparency initiatives. Previously, Anita was the Chief Financial Officer of Epic Systems Corporation, an electronic medical records company that serves more than 150 million Americans. In the past, Anita has also served on the boards of Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco (Los Angeles), Dignity Health Foundation and Allscripts (Nasdaq: MDRX).

    Education

    Anita holds a B.S. degree in Engineering and an M.B.A. from The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania.
