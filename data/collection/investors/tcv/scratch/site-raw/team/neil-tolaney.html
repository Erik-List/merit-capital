    Neil Tolaney

    General Partner

    Location

    Menlo Park

    Year Joined

    2020

    Background

    Neil first joined TCV in 2011 and rejoined in 2020. He focuses on investments that serve consumers and SMEs across digital entertainment, digital health, online fitness, prosumer empowerment, e-commerce, fintech, edtech and online travel. Neil brings both investment and operating experience specifically in the consumer internet space. He is currently involved with investments in Adevinta, ByteDance, GoodRx (NASDAQ: GDRX), Grow Therapy, Hotmart, LegalZoom (NASDAQ: LZ), Mercury, onX, Strava, and Zillow (NASDAQ: Z). He was previously involved with TCV’s investments in AppNexus (acquired by AT&T), Brex (acquired by Capital One, NYSE: COF), Facebook (NASDAQ: META), Hinge Health (NYSE: HNGE), Instacart (NASDAQ: CART), and Minted. Most recently, Neil was a Partner at Francisco Partners. Prior to that, he was Managing Director of PersonalizationMall.com, an e-commerce direct marketer and category leader of personalized gifts that was acquired by Bed Bath and Beyond. He previously served as Director of Strategic Development at LegalZoom (NASDAQ: LZ).

    Interests

    Neil and his wife spend most of their free time trying to keep up with their four energetic young children, who ensure there is never a dull moment at home!

    Education

    Neil earned his BA degrees in Economics and Political Science, magna cum laude, from Yale University, where he was on the varsity tennis team, and his MBA from Harvard Business School.
