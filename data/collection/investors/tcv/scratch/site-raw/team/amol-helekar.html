    Amol Helekar

    General Partner

    Location

    New York

    Year Joined

    2009

    Background

    Amol joined TCV in 2009 and focuses on investments in software, fintech, tech-enabled services, and AI. He is currently involved with investments in Built, Clio, First Due, Jane, Payoneer (NASDAQ: PAYO), and Trulioo. He was previously involved with Avetta, AxiomSL, Evisort, OneSource Virtual (sold to TA Associates), Razorpay, and Toast (NYSE: TOST). Prior to TCV, Amol spent several years with McKinsey & Company, where he advised clients on strategy engagements in the energy, financial services, and technology industries.

    Interests

    Amol was born in Goa, India, and grew up in Houston. He enjoys traveling, biking, and going for strolls around New York with his wife and young son.

    Education

    Amol received his M.B.A. from Harvard Business School and graduated with a B.A. in Economics from Stanford University.
