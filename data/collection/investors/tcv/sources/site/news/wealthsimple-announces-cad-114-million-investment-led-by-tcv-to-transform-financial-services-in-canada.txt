Wealthsimple announces CAD $114 million investment led by TCV to transform financial services in Canada - TCV News Wealthsimple announces CAD $114 million investment led by TCV to transform financial services in Canada October 14, 2020 Share TORONTO,&nbsp;Oct. 14, 2020&nbsp;/CNW/ &#8211;&nbsp;Wealthsimple, the company behind&nbsp;Canada&#8217;s&nbsp;leading digital investing and stock trading platforms, today announced it has raised CAD&nbsp;$114 million&nbsp;on a valuation of CAD&nbsp;$1.4 billion. The investment was led by TCV, one of the largest growth equity investors focused on technology, alongside Greylock, Meritech, Two Sigma Ventures and existing investor Allianz X. Concurrent with the funding,&nbsp;David Yuan, General Partner at TCV, will join Wealthsimple&#8217;s Board of Directors. Founded in 2014, Wealthsimple has evolved the financial services industry in&nbsp;Canada&nbsp;by bringing smart, simple and affordable financial products and services to market; today over 1.5 million Canadians use Wealthsimple products. The company is known for its client-centric approach to financial products, which include automated investing, commission-free stock and crypto trading, a savings account, and a tax filing software. Wealthsimple will use the new capital to expand its market position, build out its product suite, and grow its team in&nbsp;Canada. &#8220;Our growth over the past six years shows how ready Canadians, and especially younger Canadians, are for a new model of financial services designed around their needs. But too many people still don&#8217;t have access to great financial products or are paying too much for them,&#8221; said&nbsp;Mike Katchen, co-founder and CEO of Wealthsimple. &#8220;There&#8217;s still so much room to grow, and to have investors of this caliber join us is an incredible vote of confidence in both our mission and our ability to deliver on it.&#8221; &#8220;We have been watching Wealthsimple&#8217;s rise in the Canadian market and love the way the company is bringing simplicity, humanity, and delight to personal finance,&#8221; said&nbsp;David Yuan, General Partner, TCV. &#8220;TCV is focused on businesses that have the potential to transform industries, and we are thrilled to work with Mike and the Wealthsimple team to build a leader in financial services and an important consumer platform.&#8221; TCV invests in tech-focused companies with an ambition to become market leaders. The growth equity firm has backed over 350 technology companies, including Airbnb, Klarna, Netflix, Nubank, Peloton, Revolut, Spotify, and Zillow. Greylock, a longstanding venture capital firm that focuses on enterprise and consumer software, has invested in companies including LinkedIn and Airbnb, and has partnered with over 180 companies through IPO, while Meritech has partnered with 200 companies globally including Salesforce and Snowflake. The investment also marks the first time that Meritech has invested in the Canadian market. About Wealthsimple Wealthsimple is a financial company on a mission to help everyone achieve financial freedom by providing products and advice that are accessible and affordable. Using smart technology, Wealthsimple takes financial services that are often confusing, opaque and expensive and makes them simple, transparent, and low-cost. The company was founded by a team of financial experts and technology entrepreneurs, and is headquartered in&nbsp;Toronto, Canada. To learn more, visit&nbsp; www.wealthsimple.com . About TCV Founded in 1995, TCV provides capital to growth-stage private and public companies in the technology industry. Since its inception, TCV has invested over&nbsp;$14 billion&nbsp;in leading technology companies, including more than&nbsp;$2 billion&nbsp;in fintech, and has helped guide CEOs through more than 120 IPOs and strategic acquisitions. TCV is headquartered in&nbsp;Menlo Park, California, with offices in&nbsp;New York&nbsp;and&nbsp;London.
