Klarna announces $650M funding round to further accelerate global growth - TCV News Klarna announces $650M funding round to further accelerate global growth September 15, 2020 Share NEW YORK,&nbsp;Sept. 15, 2020&nbsp;/PRNewswire/ &#8212; Klarna, the leading global payments and shopping service, is pleased to announce it has raised&nbsp;$650 million&nbsp;in an equity funding round, at a post money valuation of&nbsp;$10.65 billion, which ranks Klarna as the highest-valued private fintech in&nbsp;Europe&nbsp;and now the 4th highest worldwide.&nbsp;&nbsp; The funding round is led by&nbsp;Silver Lake, the global leader in technology investing, with more than&nbsp;$60 billion&nbsp;in combined assets under management, alongside GIC &#8211;&nbsp;Singapore&#8217;s&nbsp;sovereign wealth fund &#8211; as well as funds and accounts managed by BlackRock and HMI Capital. Concurrently, Merian Chrysalis, TCV, Northzone and Bonnier have acquired shares from existing shareholders. They will join current investors such as Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer, Permira, Commonwealth Bank of&nbsp;Australia, Bestseller Group and Ant Group in supporting Klarna&#8217;s future growth. The funding will help Klarna further invest in its unique shopping offering, continue to grow its global presence, and accelerate its strong momentum across all markets, especially in the US where the company is growing particularly rapidly and now has more than 9 million consumers.&nbsp; The uniqueness of Klarna&#8217;s offer &#8211; providing a healthier, simpler and smarter alternative to credit cards and a broad range of services to enable a superior shopping journey &#8211; continues to drive rapid consumer adoption and loyalty with more than 90 million consumers worldwide. This is at a time when consumers are actively turning away from revolving credit lines and inferior retail experiences towards services that better meet their needs. Klarna&#8217;s direct to consumer app, which enables users to shop at any store or brand online with installment payment options, is strongly resonating as consumers have become more focused on convenience, value and control. The app has more than 12 million monthly active users worldwide, with 55,000 daily downloads, significantly outpacing its nearest competitor with almost 3x as many downloads over the last year. In the app, consumers can also now wishlist their favourite items, access unique discounts, set up price-drop notifications and track spending and deliveries intelligently. The app also features the world&#8217;s first buy now, pay later shopper loyalty program, Vibe, which rewards consumers who pay on time, to encourage responsible spending. Vibe is currently available to consumers in the US and will soon be launched in additional markets.&nbsp;&nbsp; In the context of the current accelerated switch to online retail and evolving consumer preferences, Klarna has seen a surge in demand, adding more than 35,000 new retailers during the first half of 2020 to its network of more than 200,000 retail partners including Sephora, Groupon, SHEIN,&nbsp;Charlotte Tilbury, Vans, The North Face,&nbsp;Ted Baker, Timberland and&nbsp;Ralph Lauren. Klarna is the pay later partner of choice for the top 100 highest grossing merchants in the US and will soon go live with more ahead of the busiest shopping season. A recent&nbsp; McKinsey &amp; Company &nbsp;consumer survey found that more than 75% of consumers have tried new brands, places to shop or methods of shopping during COVID-19 and that 82% of those who have tried a new digital shopping method intend to continue using it even after the crisis ends. This is reflected in strong business results for Klarna: volume and revenue for the first half of 2020 grew 44% and 36% year-on-year to more than&nbsp;$22 billion&nbsp;and&nbsp;$466 million&nbsp;respectively.&nbsp;&nbsp; Sebastian Siemiatkowski, co-founder and CEO of Klarna: &nbsp;&#8220; We are at a true inflection point in both retail and finance. The shift to online retail is now truly supercharged and there is a very tangible change in the behaviour of consumers who are now actively seeking services which offer convenience, flexibility and control in how they pay and an overall superior shopping experience. Klarna&#8217;s unique proposition, consumer preference and global retailer network will prove an excellent platform for further growth. Klarna&#8217;s retail partners benefit from incremental traffic and dramatically improved customer conversion. About Klarna We make shopping smooth. With Klarna, consumers can buy now and pay later, so they can get what they need today. Klarna&#8217;s offering to consumers and retailers includes payments, social shopping and personal finances. Over 200,000 retail partners, including H&amp;M, IKEA, Expedia Group, Samsung, ASOS, Peloton, Abercrombie &amp; Fitch, Nike and AliExpress, have enabled Klarna&#8217;s innovative shopping experience online and in-store. Klarna is ranked number five on the 2020 CNBC Disruptor 50 list, is the most highly valued private fintech in&nbsp;Europe&nbsp;and the fourth highest globally, with a valuation of&nbsp;$10.65 billion. Klarna was founded in 2005, has over 3,500 employees and is active in 17 countries. Klarna has been backed by Sequoia Capital since 2010 and more recently, Dragoneer, Bestseller Group, Permira, Visa,&nbsp; Atomico, Ant Group and&nbsp;Silver Lake, HMI Capital, TCV amongst others. For more information, visit&nbsp; klarna.com .
