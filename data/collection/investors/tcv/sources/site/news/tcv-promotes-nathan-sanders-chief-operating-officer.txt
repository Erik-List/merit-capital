TCV Promotes Nathan Sanders to Chief Operating Officer - TCV News &#8226; March 26, 2018 TCV Promotes Nathan Sanders to Chief Operating Officer Share The creation of the COO role and the promotion of Nathan into this position are natural steps in the continued growth of the firm. As a General Partner and senior member of TCV, Nathan’s insights as Head of Investor Relations and his in-depth understanding of the business make him ideally suited to oversee the evolution of the firm’s operations. In his expanded role, Nathan will oversee TCV’s Finance, Marketing, Human Capital, and Information Technology functions, as well as continue to run TCV’s Investor Relations activities. TCV is committed to enhancing the firm’s operational platform to create a lasting franchise and to better serve our stakeholders. Nathan’s promotion is further evidence of the importance of this goal and our ongoing commitment. Nathan Sanders Nathan joined TCV in 2014, bringing a combination of investment, technology, and management experience. Prior to joining TCV, Nathan was a director at Bain Capital, with roles on both the private equity investment team and the investor relations team. Previously, he was an Associate Partner at McKinsey &amp; Company in Palo Alto, where he advised clients on a range of strategy and finance-related priorities. He has also held positions at Accenture and a startup technology company. Nathan received an M.B.A. with distinction from Harvard Business School and a B.S. in MIS and Entrepreneurship from the University of Arizona. We are excited about building on TCV’s momentum and broadening our growth. We congratulate Nathan on his success to date and look forward to his contributions at TCV for many years to come.
