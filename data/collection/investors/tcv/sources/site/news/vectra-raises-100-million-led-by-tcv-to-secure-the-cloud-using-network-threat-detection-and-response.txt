Vectra raises $100 million led by TCV to secure the cloud using network threat detection and response - TCV News &#8226; June 10, 2019 Vectra raises $100 million led by TCV to secure the cloud using network threat detection and response Financing to drive further global expansion in the $12.7 billion* cloud security market. Share SAN JOSE, Calif.,&nbsp;June 10, 2019&nbsp;/PRNewswire/ &#8212;&nbsp; Vectra , the leader in network threat detection and response (NDR), today closed a&nbsp;$100 million&nbsp;round of funding led by&nbsp; TCV , one of the largest growth equity firms backing private and public technology companies.&nbsp; Existing investors &nbsp;also participated in the funding round, bringing the company&#8217;s total funding to date to more than&nbsp;$200 million. Vectra will use the investment to accelerate global market expansion and R&amp;D innovation, solidifying its Cognito platform as the market-leading solution for artificial intelligence (AI)-driven cloud security using NDR. The cloud has critical security gaps that leave organizations vulnerable. Cyberattackers take advantage of these gaps without leaving a trail of evidence. Underscoring this risk, a recent survey by the SANS Institute found that one in five businesses had serious unauthorized access to their cloud environments this past year alone, and many more were unknowingly breached. The Cognito platform addresses these security gaps by providing 360-degree visibility into cloud, data center, user and internet-of-things (IoT) infrastructure, leaving attackers with nowhere to hide. &#8220;TCV has an extensive track record of partnering with enterprise security companies, including Rapid7 and Splunk, from growth stage to public,&#8221; said&nbsp;Tim McAdam, general partner at TCV and a member of the Vectra board of directors. &#8220;In our research on the category, it became clear to us that Vectra was rapidly gaining momentum with customers by rethinking the way enterprises view both network and cloud security. The Vectra Cognito platform is poised to become requisite in the security infrastructure of multinational enterprises and midsize businesses alike.&#8221; &#8220;The cloud has inherent security blind spots, making it imperative to eliminate cyber-risks as enterprises move their business to the cloud,&#8221; said&nbsp;Hitesh Sheth, president and chief executive officer at Vectra. &#8220;The Cognito platform enables them to stop hidden cyberattacks in the cloud. We look forward to partnering with TCV and our existing investors as we continue our rapid growth.&#8221; Vectra experienced 104% growth in annual recurring revenue in 2018 compared to 2017. The company will continue to ramp up initiatives aimed at addressing the global deficit in cloud security, innovating on its existing platform and expanding its global customer base. *&nbsp; &#8220; Cloud Security Solutions Forecast , &nbsp;2018 to 2023 &#8221; by Forrester Research, Inc. About Vectra Vectra ® &nbsp;is the leader in network detection and response – from cloud and data center workloads to user and IoT devices. Its Cognito ® &nbsp;platform accelerates threat detection and investigation using AI to enrich network metadata it collects and stores with the right context to detect, hunt and investigate known and unknown threats in real time. Vectra offers three applications on the Cognito platform to address high-priority use cases. Cognito Stream &#x2122; &nbsp;sends security-enriched metadata to data lakes and SIEMs. Cognito Recall &#x2122; &nbsp;is a cloud-based application to store and investigate threats in enriched metadata. And Cognito Detect &#x2122; &nbsp;uses AI to reveal and prioritize hidden and unknown attackers at speed. For more information, visit&nbsp; vectra.ai .
