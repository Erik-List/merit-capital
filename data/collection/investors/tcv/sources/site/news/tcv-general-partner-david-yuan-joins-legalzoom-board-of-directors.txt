TCV General Partner David Yuan Joins LegalZoom Board of Directors - TCV News &#8226; October 30, 2018 TCV General Partner David Yuan Joins LegalZoom Board of Directors Share GLENDALE, Calif.&#8211;( BUSINESS WIRE )&#8211;LegalZoom, the leading provider of legal solutions for small businesses and families, announced that David Yuan, a General Partner at TCV, has joined its board of directors as part of an investment by TCV in the company. Drawing on TCV’s deep experience investing in and advising companies serving small and midsized businesses (SMB), such as GoDaddy, LinkedIn and Xero, Yuan will help guide LegalZoom through its next phase of growth. “David is a strategic thinker focused on driving growth at scale with a deep understanding of SMB subscription models,” commented LegalZoom CEO John Suh. “We’re excited by the energy and insight he will bring to our Board as we enter the next phase of our journey to democratize law.” “I am excited and honored to work with John Suh and the LegalZoom team,” added David Yuan. “LegalZoom is empowering millions of small businesses and consumers with delightful product experiences and services. We believe LegalZoom is the category leader and building an important and highly valuable technology franchise.” About LegalZoom LegalZoom is a trusted platform of people and technology that gives the world access to consistently high quality legal solutions. As the nation’s leading provider, LegalZoom empowers millions of people to protect what matters most to them. Whether it’s helping small business owners or families, LegalZoom delivers high touch, high service legal solutions that help customers be more informed and secure in their legal decisions. LegalZoom services customers in the US and the UK and is now over 1,200 employees strong.
