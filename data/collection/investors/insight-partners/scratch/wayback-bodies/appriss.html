Overview

Appriss provides data and technology solutions that help keep communities across the United States safe and informed. Appriss serves more than 5,000 state, local, and federal agencies across all 50 states as well as thousands of private sector clients in healthcare, retail, insurance, and other verticals. Its software and data products help those customers prevent fraud, reduce risk, fight crime, increase safety, and save lives. Appriss is headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky and has more than 400 employees nationwide.
