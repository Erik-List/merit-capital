Overview

Aelita Software provides systems management solutions to organizations that rely on Microsoft Windows technologies. Aelita’s proven expertise with Active Directory and Exchange helps customers improve productivity, system availability and security. IT professionals choose Aelita solutions to administer, migrate, recover and audit these critical systems. The company’s customers and partners include Bristol-Myers Squibb, Countrywide Home Loans, HMS Host (formerly known as Host Marriott Services), Kmart Corporation, Pitney Bowes, Textron, Inc., Hewlett-Packard and Microsoft. Aelita is a global organization with headquarters in Columbus, Ohio.
