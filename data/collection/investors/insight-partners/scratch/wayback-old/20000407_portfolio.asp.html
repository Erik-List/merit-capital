PORTFOLIO COMPANIES

Since its founding in 1995, Insight Capital Partners has pursued an investment strategy centered on the application of technology to the solution of complex business problems. Leveraging this strength, Insight is now at the forefront of the business-to-business e-commerce revolution. With a portfolio of companies exclusively focused on B2B e-commerce and business applications, Insight Capital has unparalleled experience and deep insight into the strategy and execution of building market-leading B2B companies.

With over 50 investments in the B2B e-commerce space, Insight has systematically organized its investments along two dimensions: industry vertical and functional area. Insight is a leading investor in the following B2B e-commerce verticals: manufacturing, telecommunications, financial services, energy, retail, and healthcare. In addition, Insight makes a number of investments that focus on marketing/eCRM solutions, infrastructure software and services.
