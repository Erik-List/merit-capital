MANUFACTURING

Click Commerce
CEO: Mike Ferro
Phone: 312.482.9006
www.clickcommerce.com
Click Commerce, based in Chicago, IL, is the market leader in providing Collaborative Commerce solutions for Fortune 1000 manufacturers. Collaborative Commerce enables manufacturers to automate information flows and transactions within a multi-channel distribution network, including manufacturers, distributors, dealers, service groups, and end consumers. Click's solutions allow manufacturers to increase top-line revenue while decreasing operating costs, and enable the dealer/distributor network to collaborate with the manufacturers in real-time over the internet 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

iMany (formerly SCC)
CEO: Leigh Powell
Phone: 207.774.3244
www.imany.com
iMany, based in Portland, Maine, is the leading trading exchange in the pharmaceutical and medical/surgical arena, allowing partners in the supply chain to conduct business-to-business e-commerce. The Company provides innovative contract management and optimization software solutions for pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical/surgical manufacturers and packaged goods manufacturers and distributors.

LPA Software
CEO: Michael Fabiaschi
Phone: 716.248.9600
www.lpa.com
LPA Software, based in Rochester, NY, is a leading provider of e-commerce and supply chain management solutions to the post-production service parts market. LPA's Vision software allows customers to forecast demand, manage spare parts inventory, and plan repair and deployment to manage the post-production life-cycle of durable goods. LPA has created business-to-business trading exchanges of complex components for service organizations. Customers include AT&T, Allied Signal, Bell Atlantic, Eastman Kodak, and Xerox.

Optum Software
CEO: David Simbari
Phone: 914.697.4766
www.optum.com
Optum, headquartered in White Plains, NY, is a leading supply chain execution solutions provider, helping manufacturers, distributors, and retailers form best-practice competency in e-Fulfillment, e-Commerce, and e-Services. Optum's product offerings allow a customer to manage and execute the supply chain beginning from the demand center of the manufacturer or distributor leading to fulfillment at the end-user business or consumer customer. Optum was formed by the merger of Metasys and Optum Software, two Insight portfolio companies. Optum's customers include Webvan, W.W. Grainger, and the Home Shopping Network.

Nistevo (formerly Transview)
CEO: Kevin Lynch
Phone: 612.893.9600
www.nistevo.com
Nistevo Corporation, located in Minneapolis, MN, provides Internet based business-to-business e-commerce solutions for logistics services. The Company's solution, e-Logistix, is a robust, scalable web application that operates between trading partners as a virtual private network and is used by supply chain participants, including shippers and carriers, to collaboratively manage the procurement and execution of logistics services.

SeeCommerce (formerly VIT)
CEO: Paul Albright
Phone: 650-213-1800
