Investment Philosophy

We believe that the tech investing ecosystem has fundamentally changed over the past few years, whereby investing capital alone is no longer sufficient.  Entrepreneurs want to work only with the most valuable investors – those who can provide not only capital, but also strategy, execution capability, and a support infrastructure that helps companies build value.

At Lakestar we believe in a very “hands-on”, or entrepreneurial, approach to investing.  In addition to strategic guidance and introductions, we can offer entrepreneurs support in critical business functions, such as human resources, finance, technology, and online marketing.

We invest only in what we know and where we can add value, and we strongly believe we can add significant value to Internet business models. We would welcome the opportunity to speak to entrepreneurs and investment partners on new opportunities.
