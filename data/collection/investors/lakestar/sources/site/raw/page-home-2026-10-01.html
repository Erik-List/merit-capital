About

Our view of a changing world

Lakestar is the investment firm founded by Dr. Klaus Hommels in 2012.

It has over $6 billion in assets under management, on a platform that spans institutional venture capital funds and personal investments across stages and geographies.

We exist because the environment in which technology is built, and decisions are made, has fundamentally changed.

We no longer live in a world where technology is neutral. And we no longer live in a world where sovereignty is someone else’s problem.

Today, entrepreneurial decisions collide with geopolitical reality, every single day.

Philosophy

A Sharper Mission.

We are focused on maximising the potential of our existing portfolio and building targeted investment products that help our companies grow, supporting Europe’s sovereignty and prosperity.

Less noise. More commitment. More time where it matters: with founders.

Team
