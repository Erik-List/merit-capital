Gowalla

The company develops software that enriches on-line social interactions. It is the creator of the location-based social network Gowalla, where users merge real-world and on-line experiences by communicating with friends about their environments, learning about new locations, and sharing virtual goods.

Over 150,000 real-world locations in over 8,500 cities have been shared via Gowalla, and the site attracted more than 50,000 users in the ten weeks following its offical launch. The company is also the creator of the cult-hit card-trading game PackRat, a pioneering social game that boasts the one of the most active player communities on Facebook.
