# Set aside: OGE Form 278e (Kenneth Howery, 2025)

Set aside on 2026-10-06 and not used by any layer of this run. The extract the
earlier session saved (sources/web/howery-2025-fund-notes.json) was deleted with
the copies this session made; the report is public at
https://s3.documentcloud.org/documents/25971902/howery-kenneth-final278.pdf
(endnotes, pages 27 to 29) and can be re-read in minutes if Erik rules it usable.

The earlier session saved three pages of Ken Howery's 2025 public financial
disclosure (OGE Form 278e, read from a DocumentCloud copy), whose endnotes list
the remaining holdings of The Founders Fund I to IV. It is the most complete
fund-level holdings list this run found.

It is set aside because the Office of Government Ethics states, on the page
that serves these reports, that under the Ethics in Government Act (5 U.S.C.
13107(c), formerly app. 105(c)) it is unlawful to obtain or use a report "for
any commercial purpose, other than by news and communications media for
dissemination to the general public". Whether a published investor data
platform is such a use is a legal question for Erik, not a research question
for the run.

Until Erik rules: no row, verdict, citation or classification in this book
rests on the report. Companies it names are in the book only where another
layer (the firm's site, the archive, SEC filings, the press) shows them on its
own.
