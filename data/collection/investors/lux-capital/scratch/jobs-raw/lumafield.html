About

Lumafield develops industrial computed tomography (CT) solutions for non-destructive testing and inspection for engineers. Its Neptune X-ray CT scanner provides detailed imaging for engineers to analyze internal structures and detect defects in various materials. The Voyager software platform enables cloud-based AI-powered analysis, offering automated inspections and collaboration tools. Triton, its high-speed CT system, is designed for rapid scanning in high-volume manufacturing environments. Lumafield’s solutions serve industries such as Aerospace, Automotive, Electronics, and Medical Devices, helping improve product quality and reliability.
