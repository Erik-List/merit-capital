# Cherry Ventures — investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../COLLECTION.md` to Cherry Ventures
(slug `cherry`, display name always **Cherry Ventures**). The judge cites these
rulings per line. Phase 0 (PRO-73) was **confirmed by Erik 2026-09-04** as five
rulings; the facts under them were model priors and were re-verified at kickoff
2026-09-04 (this file records the verified facts). Written under the derivation
protocol of COLLECTION.md §1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to
verified facts, no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's), and
**run-time standing rulings** (dated, flagged for review).

**The structural fact of this run:** Cherry Ventures is the German edition of
the EU thin-data test on a smaller, younger, seed-stage book. The firm publishes
a portfolio list (the 2026 Webflow site: 94 company pages, 90 on the list, 19
tagged EXIT), the 2024–2025 site's Sanity CMS is still publicly queryable (86
company documents with dates, founders and sector tags, plus 108 dated
articles), seven archived WordPress listing years 2016–2023 carry the firm's own
investment dates, a 2016–2019 stage label, sector classes and exit flags, and
the Getro job board at talent.cherry.vc lists 98 companies. Its SEC footprint is
fund-side only: an active exempt reporting adviser (Form ADV with Section 7.B),
two US feeder L.P.s and one German KG filing Form D; no issuer-side ownership
filing, and the EDGAR full-text search for "Cherry Ventures" over all years
returns nothing beyond those Form Ds. The German registers (Handelsregister via
North Data, Companies House, Bolagsverket) give the entity set with dates,
general partners and limited partners — 44 entities — and no portfolio.
