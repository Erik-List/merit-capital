# HV Capital: investor-specific decisions

Applies the membership rules of `../../../COLLECTION.md` to HV Capital (slug
`hv-capital`, display name always **HV Capital**; "Holtzbrinck Ventures" from
April 2000, "HV Holtzbrinck Ventures" from the 2010 spin-off, "HV Capital" from
the 2020 rebrand). The judge cites these rulings per line. Written at kickoff
2026-09-29 (PRO-104, sub-issue of the PRO-87 wave) under the derivation protocol
of COLLECTION.md s1: **derivations** (a standing test applied to verified facts;
no confirmation needed, vetoable), **deltas** (Erik's), and **run-time standing
rulings** (dated, class-wide, flagged for review). PRO-104 named four deltas; all
four are derived below from verified facts (D2 the lineage, D3 the rename, D9 the
German recipe, D10 the retired pre-window constant), applied, and flagged.

**The structural fact of this run:** HV is a Munich and Berlin early-to-growth
firm whose portfolio is overwhelmingly German and European and files nothing
with the SEC, while its own fund side is visible there (two exempt reporting
advisers with Section 7.B tables, six fund Form D filers, one fund 13G filer).
What replaces the portfolio-side Form D is the firm's own record, which is
unusually complete: the live export (154 companies with the firm's own entry
year, fund, entry stage and status), the archived 2022-2025 Storyblok lists (223
companies including exits back to 1999, each with an entry DATE and the deal
partners), the 2015-2017 WordPress tiles (founded year and investment-start year
per company, the representative partner), and the 2006-2015 holtzbrinck-ventures.com
pages (the whole current and former portfolio in every page's navigation, with a
paragraph per company).

- **D9: German recipe (PRO-104 delta, derived).** Cherry's conventions apply:
  insolvency followed by an asset sale reads `acquired` with a successor note;
  a filing with no sale yet reads `defunct` (Erik, 2026-09-07); the home
  register's dissolution or insolvency flag is filing-grade `defunct` evidence.
  Non-USD figures convert at the ECB rate of their own date (`fx-convert.py`);
  a listing outside SEC registration (Zalando, Delivery Hero, HelloFresh, Home24,
  Westwing on Xetra) takes a web-cited market cap.

- **D10: No entry-date window (PRO-104 delta, derived).** The issue expected "a
  pre-window slice under the 2006 constant"; the constant was removed on
  2026-09-19 (PRO-137). A 2000 entry is judged on the same evidence as a 2024
  one, and where the record does not give the round the stage reads
  `undetermined`.

- **D11: The firm's own stage field (COLLECTION.md s5).** The export's "Stage at
  Entry" (early / growth) is the firm's claim about the stage IT entered at:
  eligible. `growth` collapses onto Growth and labels once scored; `early`
  straddles Pre-Seed, Seed and Series A and is an anchor like a bare year. The
  export's "Stage" column and the Storyblok `current_stage` are the company's
  CURRENT stage (early / growth / acquired / ipo / exit): never an entry label,
  status evidence at most. The 2015-2017 tiles' investment-start year, the
  Storyblok `entry_date` and the export's "HV Entry" are the firm's own dated
  entry claims: `first_seen` / `entry_date` anchors, read as the close year
  (Menlo rule), checked against documented deals before they date a graduation.
