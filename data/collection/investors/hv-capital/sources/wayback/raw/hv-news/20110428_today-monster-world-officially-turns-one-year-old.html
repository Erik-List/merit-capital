Today, Monster World officially turns one year old!

Launched on April 27th, 2010, Monster World has been played by more than 20 million people from 231 nations since April 2010. A dedicated team of 15 launch an improved version of the game every week, with more than 56 iterated versions since the public launch. wooga is constantly seeking to serve the best gaming experience possible, and the 1.1 million daily players, each logging in more than 8 times every day, really encourage us. Remarkable: Monster World has it’s all time high in terms of monthly active Users today, one year after it’s first launch and it doesn’t stop growing. Since its launch, 594 unique, decorative items – from tree-houses and castles to pyramids and space satellites – have been invented and implemented by the creative team. 

 

Designed from the bottom up as a social game, a major portion of the Monster World gameplay is to play with your friends: Our user have oiled each other’s robots more than 1.6 billion times, have fed their hungry helpers 930 million times and collected 1 billion bags of gold in their friend’s gardens.

Users in Monster World manufacture and sell goods. For example, users have produced more than 60 million pieces of candy in their candy factories, and crafted over 3.7 million spiky lollipops – ouch.

Monster World is available wherever Facebook is and is localized in seven languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, and Turkish).

The last year was exciting for both Monster World and wooga as a whole. It has been a year with incredible growth, during which wooga grew from a small developber in Berlin to the fourth largest in the world. The Monster World team has had a huge impact on the success of wooga and we wanted to thank them – that’s why we threw a little party for the team in the office.
