Audible.de

Audible is the leading download platform for audio books and audio content, such as spoken newspapers, magazines, TV and radio programs and podcasts. Audible offers more than 25,000 German, English, Spanish and French titles from over 400 publishers. Audible maintains an exclusive worldwide partnership with Apple, who also offers the full spectrum of audio books in their iTunes Store. Other benefits include the compatibility of all iPods with the Audible file format, which was especially developed for audio books. Incidentally, this is the only third party format supported by the iPod.

More than 300,000 users have already downloaded audio content from www.audible.de to either their PC or Mac. The audio files can then be transferred to mobile devices such as MP3 players, Smartphones and PDAs. Audio content downloaded from Audible can also be played directly on the PC/ Mac or can be burnt on CD. The Audible Air service also allows audio books and magazines to be downloaded straight to mobile phones, thereby bypassing the PC. This means that the audio book fan can finally enjoy all the titles that he ever wanted to read while allowing him to keep his digital library, at hand in his jacket pocket. 

The Audible GmbH, based in Munich, was founded on August 30th 2004 as a joint venture between several strong partners. These include Audible Inc. (NASDAQ: ADBL), Holtzbrinck Ventures GmbH, the publishing group Random House (Bertelsmann) as well as the Lübbe publishing house.
