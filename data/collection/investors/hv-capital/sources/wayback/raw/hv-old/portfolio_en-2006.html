Fields of Investment

Our portfolio concentrates on media-oriented businesses with digital technologies (internet, mobile communication, broadband). We are interested in products and services as well as technological applications and solutions.

  • Audible.deAudible links the century old tradition of story telling with the most modern of communication techniques - the Internet, and offers audio files for download.
  • e-fellows.nete-fellows.net awards the first online scholarships to the most gifted and successful students in Europe.
  • Experteer Experteer is the first premium career service in the German-speaking sector of the Internet. The company arranges career opportunities for highly qualified specialist and executive staff.
  • GameDuellGameDuell makes use of the technical possibilities of gaming networks and is Germany’s leading online gaming website.
  • Gute Laune TVGute Laune TV (www.gutelaunetv.de) broadcasts German pop and folk music, as well as quiz programs, live concerts, competitions and much, much more.
  • InnofactInnofact offers its customers a complete portfolio of innovative market research solutions.
  • MindMaticsMindmatics is one of the leading mobile service providers in Europe and offers a one-stop complete and integrated mobile communications service.
  • MY-HAMMERMy-Hammer is the leading auction company within Germany and Austria for trade and services auctions.
  • newtron The Newtron AG supports its industrial clients in managing their relationships with suppliers by using a web-based technology platform.
  • PanelbizPanelbiz offers online market research services. Central to panelbiz is “Meinungsplatz”, the largest market research panel in Germany.
  • ParshipParship is Europe’s leading match making service thanks to its scientifically based partnership tests.
  • Six Offene Systeme The Six Offene Systeme GmbH is a leading supplier of open source enterprise content management products.
  • studiVZstudiVZ is Europe’s largest and fastest growing student network.
