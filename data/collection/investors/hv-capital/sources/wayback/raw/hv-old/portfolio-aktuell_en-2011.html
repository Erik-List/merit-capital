Groupon

Every day Groupon offers exciting new deals in a large number of German and European cities.Inexpensive services are offered in the areas of recreation, restaurants, wellness and entertainment.

Discounts of up to 80% are available.If the minimum number of people interested in a deal sign up for it before it expires, it goes ahead.Purchased coupons can be redeemed immediately.This makes Groupon appealing to both customers and companies wanting to increase their level of awareness or reach new audiences with their offers.

CityDeal GmbH was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Berlin.Groupon Inc. acquired all shares in the company in May 2010.Since then the company has also been operating under the Groupon banner.
