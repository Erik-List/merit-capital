Partners of Holtzbrinck Ventures

Holtzbrinck Ventures an extensive network of affiliated partner companies. This network is made up of the co-partners of Holtzbrinck Ventures and friendly venture capital firms. Verlagsgruppe Georg von Holtzbrinck (Publishing Group)The publishing group Georg von Holtzbrinck is one of the largest German media companies. Sales figures for 2005 total 2,1 billion Euro, of which 51% came from overseas. Georg von Holtzbrinck founded the family business in Stuttgart in 1948. Emerging from Deutscher Bücherbund (dominant German book club), today many well known firms belong to the Holtzbrinck publishing group (www.holtzbrinck.de). These include the Handelsblatt, Die Zeit, Rowohlt, S. Fischer, Spektrum der Wissenschaft as well as the worldwide Macmillan Group’s Nature Publishing.
www.holtzbrinck.de

Holtzbrinck NetworksHoltzbrinck Networks combines the strategic Internet shareholding of the publishing group Georg von Holtzbrinck. Thereby, our emphasis is not on selling the company at a later date, rather on building up new fields of business and looking after the investments or the acquisition of new internet companies.
www.holtzbrinck-networks.de

Holtzbrinck eLabHoltzbrinck eLab, located in Munich, is a subsidiary of the publishing group Georg von Holtzbrinck, and started business on January 1st 2006. The company gathers promising ideas for user-oriented Internet and mobile applications. These are then systematically tested with the participation of users at an early stage. When a new application receives positive feedback, the concept will be established in the market.
www.holtzbrinck-elab
