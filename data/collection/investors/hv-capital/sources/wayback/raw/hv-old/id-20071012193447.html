David Kuczek

David Kuczek
Investment Manager

E-mail: david.kuczek@holtzbrinck.net

Through various entrepreneurial activities during the course of his studies David earned a valueable insight into the dynamics of young internet start-ups. He co-founded arasis.de, a student community, and aimido.de, a goal setting community.

Within Holtzbrinck Ventures David focuses on investments in Internet communities and serves as a trend scout.

Prior to joining Holtzbrinck Ventures and alongside his entrepreneurial activities David worked on an eLearning implementation at Sloan School of Management in Cambridge (USA) and on a global knowledge sharing project at Siemens AG in Munich.

David studied business administration at the University of Cologne and majored in corporate finance, media management, and information systems.
