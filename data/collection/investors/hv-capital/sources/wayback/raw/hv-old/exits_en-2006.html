Closed Partnerships

Investments with venture capital are limited by time, usually between three and seven years. During this time no interest must be paid. Rather, the profit is realised by exit proceeds at the end of the investment period. For this reason the Exit Strategy is crucial for Holtzbrinck Ventures and greatly influences the decision of whether to invest or not.
