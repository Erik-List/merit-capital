## Deltas (Erik's to confirm)

- **X1 — The shape guard is restated before the grind** (PRO-92's delta,
  settled in Phase 0 as the issue asks; **flagged for Erik, the run proceeds
  on it**). `verify.shape.one_b_share_max` is **0.45**, not the 0.20 default.
  IVP enters at growth rounds by design ("our work begins at critical stages
  of inflection"; "130+ IPOs out of 400 investments", the firm's own about
  text), so a large $1B+ share is the expected shape of a correct base, and
  Thrive, the closest growth-heavy precedent, closed near 39%. The ceiling is
  pre-registered, not fitted: it still fires above 45%, where the share would
  say the base holds the curated winners and has lost the quieter companies
  (which is a real risk here, because the archive that carries them is
  Common Crawl rather than the Internet Archive). `c2a_rate_max` keeps the
  0.80 default: the Seed cohort is expected to be a handful of rows, and a
  breach on a cohort that small is ruled at close with the measured cohort,
  never widened in advance.

  **The Coatue question (PRO-92's second delta item) does not arise:**
  PRO-78 has not run, so there is no crossover `entry_stage` ruling to borrow,
  and none is needed. IVP enters through private primary and secondary
  rounds, which the existing taxonomy already labels (Growth is Series B and
  every later round, secondaries included). The column needs the shape
  restatement above, not a new vocabulary.

## Run-time standing rulings

### R1 — A Common Crawl capture of ivp.com is a site listing and cites itself (2026-09-26)

Class-wide for this investor, flagged for review. A company named on an
archived ivp.com listing page (the 2013 to 2022 site's `/portfolio/...`
pages, company pages, team bios naming a portfolio company as IVP's) is
`site_listing` evidence exactly as a Wayback capture would be (§1: "a listing
on the investor's own site (any era)"). The citation is the capture itself
(the `index.commoncrawl.org` record for that URL and timestamp), never a
`web.archive.org` link, which would resolve to the exclusion page.

### R2 — The site's "Year Invested" / "Partnered" on a legacy entry is not an entry date (2026-09-26)

Class-wide for this investor, flagged for review. For companies IVP held
before its 2013 site, the year field is unreliable: the site dates Netflix
"Partnered 2006" while Netflix's 2002 prospectus shows IVP VIII a holder with
Timothy Haley on the board since June 1998, and it dates Omniture 2009, three
years after its IPO. Such a year is an anchor at most; a legacy row's entry
round comes from filings or press, and a year later than the company's IPO is
a flag for review (T3), not evidence of a post-IPO purchase on its own.

### R3 — A Growth stage may cite IVP's own dated announcement (2026-09-26)

Flagged for Erik (open ruling 7 in the report). Where IVP's own post announces
its investment on a date when every round the company had raised was Series B
or later, the entry is Growth whichever round it was, and the post is cited
(`web_verified`, confidence medium) with the round history in the note. Used
on six rows (Figma, Gamma, Robinhood, Suno, UiPath, Klarna). A bare year on
the portfolio page is not enough (R2): Uber, Vercel, Datadog and Pure Storage
stay `cite_pending`.
